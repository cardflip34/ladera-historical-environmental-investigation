# FUTURE_EVIDENCE_GATES.md

Evidence that requires private records, institutional cooperation, consent, or laboratory
work. These are **final-stage gates**. Per `CLAUDE.md` rule 8, they are logged here and do
**not** block online-first work. Records-request drafting happens only after the public-
source investigation identifies exact gaps (Phase 13). Rankings are populated at that stage.

Legend for ranking columns (filled in Phase 13): Sci. importance / Availability / Cost /
Legal difficulty / Time sensitivity / Destruction risk / Ability to change conclusions /
Privacy impact — each High / Med / Low.

## Ranking & drafted requests

The gates above are now **ranked** (scientific importance × availability × cost × legal
difficulty × time sensitivity × destruction risk × ability-to-change-conclusions × privacy
impact), and precise, gap-specific records requests for the top five are drafted, in
**`reports/evidence_gate_package.md`**. Top priorities: **G01** (CCR aggregate case data),
**G03** (residence histories, IRB), **G10** (soil sampling), **G16** (official review
results). Two gates carry **high destruction risk** and should be preserved urgently:
**G04/G05** (HOA & vendor application logs).
