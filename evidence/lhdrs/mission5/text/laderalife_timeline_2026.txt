Explore Ladera Ranch
THIS IS THE PLACE
Where you can plant roots and grow wings
Ladera Ranch was founded in 1999 on one singular ideal — to create a community that enables residents to give their families and children roots and wings.
As a result, we have created an environment that offers the charm and safety of a small town. One where neighbors wave and smile, meet in the cul-de-sacs for evening conversations, and where exclusive community events offer unique experiences and memories to last a lifetime.
This mindset is why families are attracted to our community and everything it offers. From our pet-friendly parks to our close-knit clubs, groups, and programs, Ladera Ranch is a community that proves we are better together. It’s a place where one can be playful and feel young. It’s a place where one can rejuvenate and recharge. Most importantly, it’s a place that feels like home.

Discover schools, health and wellness locations and businesses – in and around Ladera Ranch.
Ladera Ranch is comprised of three districts, six villages and unique amenities that are maintained beautifully. Local businesses range from restaurants where you'll find outdoor patios and a variety of delicious cuisine, to workout studios offering hot yoga, spinning, rowing and barre classes. Ladera is also home to a wide range of excellent healthcare options from Mission Hospital, pediatricians, dentists, chiropractors and spa businesses.
You'll also find some of the best trails for hiking, mountain biking and exploring. Our schools, tutoring options and art classes are award-winning and life-enriching.
Take time to explore your community, and shop locally to support our businesses and local economy.
