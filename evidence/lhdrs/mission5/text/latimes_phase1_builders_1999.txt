Ladera Ranch Developer Picks Phase I Builders
By
Daryl Strickland
May 4, 1999
12 AM PT
Share via

Rancho Mission Viejo LLC, master developer of the Ladera Ranch development, said it has chosen eight builders to complete the first phase of nearly 1,000 homes in the 8,100-unit planned community.
The builders and the number and type of residential units they will construct are: SeaCountry Homes, 55 single-family homes; Standard Pacific Homes, 65 single-family homes; Warmington Homes, 86 single-family homes; Brookfield Homes, 227 single-family homes; Taylor Woodrow Homes, 97 single-family homes; William Lyon Homes, 103 single-family homes; Laing Homes, 118 single-family homes; and Shea Homes, 105 attached townhomes and 106 single-family homes.
Homes in Oak Knoll, the first of five villages to be built in the 275-acre community, will be priced from about $200,000 to more than $1 million. The new development, which opens this summer, is just east of Mission Viejo.
