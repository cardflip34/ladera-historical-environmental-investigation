# Dipping-station reconstruction videos (interpretive; NOT identification of the site)

Two renders of a complete 1911-specification dipping station (USDA BAI Circular 183) on
O'Neill Ranch-type terrain, built for public awareness (Phase 2) and briefing use.

1. blender_20s_16x9.mp4 - full 3D scene (Blender 4.2, scene.py + compose.py): pens, chute,
   vat (26 ft rim / 12 ft floor / 6.5 ft deep), drip pen, barrel, windmill, mixing shed,
   ~150 head, riders; measured geometry; 1920x1080, 20 s. Dimensionally faithful.
2. ai_pass/Dipping_Station_Reel_9x16.mp4 - photoreal pass (fal.ai): each Blender keyframe
   restyled with Google Nano Banana Pro (image edit), then animated with MiniMax H3 Max
   image-to-video at 1080p, 5 s each; cut to 21.6 s 9:16 with captions and an on-screen
   "AI-assisted reconstruction, not historical footage" label. Instagram Reel format.
   Sources: photo_0N.png (stills), clip_0N.mp4 (clips), urls.txt (fal asset URLs).

Every frame carries the interpretive label. The site's depth is unmeasured and the soil is
untested; nothing here is evidence that the concrete base is a vat. fal.ai spend: ~$3.
