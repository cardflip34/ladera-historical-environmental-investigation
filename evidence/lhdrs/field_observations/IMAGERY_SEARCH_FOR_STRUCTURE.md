# Imagery search for the concrete structure — executed 2026-08-08, result: not resolvable

**Question:** can the ~12 ft × 3–4 ft concrete structure found in the Trabuco corridor be seen in
any held aerial or satellite imagery, historic or recent?

**Answer: no — and the reasons are physical, not a lack of looking.**

## What was searched

| Era | Source | Native res | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1937-38 | OC Survey 600-scale | ~1 m/px effective | structure would be ~4 × 1 px — below resolution. Corridor shows open ground, oak clusters, and **converging stock trails** near the water point |
| Jan 2004 | USGS HRO native tile 11SMT380125 | 0.3 m | corridor floor under riparian canopy; systematic detector run (below) |
| 2025 | OC Eagle 1-ft, full-res export | 0.3 m | **complete canopy closure** over the corridor — matches the ground photos of heavy brush |

## The systematic detector (2004 native)

A local-brightness rectangle detector was run over an 840 × 960 m window covering the node,
target #6 and the corridor between them: 4,554 bright components, 151 at vat scale
(2.5–12 m long, 0.5–5.5 m wide, elongated). The strongest candidates were inspected visually:

- candidates on the west edge = **building roofs** in Mission Viejo (false positives)
- candidates in the corridor = **trail segments and junctions** (false positives)
- no candidate presented as a discrete bright rectangle at the structure's scale

## Why the structure cannot show

1. **It is under canopy in every modern frame.** The ground photos show it buried in brush; the
   2025 imagery confirms full closure over the corridor.
2. **At ~3.7 × 1 m, it is at or below the effective resolution** of every frame older than 2004.
3. **Its rim is lichen-stained concrete, not bright** — the local-contrast signature a detector
   needs is weak even where the canopy thins.

## What 1937 does show

Multiple **stock trails converging** in the corridor near the 1968-mapped water point — the
classic signature of a livestock-focus location. This does not show the structure; it shows the
area functioning as handling/watering ground in the ranch era, which is consistent with — and
independent of — everything else converging on this ground.

## The one thing that changes this

**GPS coordinates taken at the structure.** With an exact position, the precise 0.3 m pixel patch
can be extracted from 2004 and 2025 and examined at native scale, and the position can be
registered against the 1937 trails, the 1948 node, the 1968 water point and the drainage. Without
coordinates, the search area is ~1 km of canopy-covered corridor.

*Statement class: documented search, negative result. No conclusion about what the structure is
follows from its absence in imagery — a 1-m-wide concrete rim under brush is expected to be
invisible.*
