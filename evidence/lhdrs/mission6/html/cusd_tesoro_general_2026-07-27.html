General
 
 
 
Tesoro High School is one of six comprehensive high schools in the Capistrano Unified School District. Tesoro opened in the fall of 2001 and currently serves 2,500+ students in grades 9-12. Students come from several south Orange County communities: Rancho Santa Margarita, Las Flores, Dove Canyon, Coto de Caza, Rancho Mission Viejo, Trabuco Canyon, Mission Viejo, and Ladera Ranch. The school benefits from a supportive and active community and maintains many vital business partnerships. Two community foundations and the Parent Teacher Student Association work diligently to support the staff and students with their work. Over 90% of parents have a college degree or some graduate/post
