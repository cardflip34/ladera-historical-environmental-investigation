# FIRST DOCTOR REVIEW PACKET (v0.1, 2026-07-23)

A 5-minute orientation for the physician's first review. Preliminary, single-reviewer, hypothesis-
neutral. Full detail in the linked files. Levels: a fact · b strong · c suggestive · d plausible ·
g unknown.

## The question
Could historical inorganic arsenic (cattle-dip era) plausibly cause the reported Ewing sarcoma (and
other cancers) in Ladera Ranch residents?

## The 6 things to know first
1. **No etiologic literature exists.** Reproducible native-database result: `arsenic AND EWSR1` = 0,
   `arsenic AND EWS-FLI1` = 0 in PubMed. The *only* arsenic–Ewing papers (n=8) are **arsenic trioxide
   as a candidate therapy** — it *kills* Ewing cells. (05_, 01_NATIVE_DB.)  → **a**
2. **Arsenic is a proven carcinogen, but of the wrong tissue type** — bladder/lung/skin (epithelial),
   plus hepatic angiosarcoma. Not bone/mesenchymal. (06_)  → **a**
3. **The mechanism arsenic is known for is the wrong mechanism.** Its dominant MOA is cytotoxicity/
   regenerative proliferation (often non-genotoxic) — not making a translocation. A minority genotoxic
   thread exists (arsenite impairs HR/BRCA1-dependent DSB repair; PMID 37762697, 19553191) but has
   never been linked to EWSR1. (07_, 14_)  → **d**
4. **Ewing's real environmental signal is "farm/pesticide/parental occupation," not arsenic** — and
   arsenic was never isolated within it. Confounders: orchard lead-arsenate, DDT, ancestry. (12_,13_)  → **c**
5. **Local exposure has never been measured.** Analog dip sites hold up to ~3,000 mg/kg arsenic decades
   on (low-lead "dip signature"), but no local soil/biomarker/tumor data exist. (08_,14_)  → **d/g**
6. **Exposure is still reconstructable — but degrading.** Baby teeth, toenails, archived newborn
   blood-spot cards, and FFPE blocks can reconstruct exposure years pre-diagnosis; soil signature is
   lost once ground is disturbed. **Preserve now.** (10_,20_PRESERVATION)  → **a/b**

## Net
Causation is **currently unsupported/unestablished**. The environmental predicate is **plausible but
unmeasured**. The defensible path is **measurement**, not assertion.

## What the physician can do that would move the evidence (design only; GATE B authorizes)
- Confirm each case's **pathology + EWSR1 fusion status/partner**; assemble residence/school and
  prenatal/parental windows **privately** (04_).
- Support a **blinded tumor** protocol (metallomics + methylation + EWSR1 breakpoint; low-mutation-
  burden caveat, 09_) and a **biomarker reconstruction** (teeth/nails/blood spots, 10_).
- Back **legal holds** on blood-spot cards and FFPE blocks, and **authorized soil speciation** before
  any ground disturbance.

## What would falsify/weaken (so the review is balanced)
Null soil dip-signature + null reconstructed exposure would substantially weaken the community-specific
case even before mechanism work. Cases dominated by germline predisposition/prior radiotherapy, or an
ancestry-standardized incidence within expected range, would also weaken it. (20_TOP_15_FALSIFIERS.)
