# DOCTOR TECHNICAL BRIEF (v0.1, 2026-07-23)

Physician-facing synthesis of the current evidence. **Preliminary, single-reviewer, hypothesis-
neutral.** Sourced findings live in 05_/06_/07_/10_. Nothing here asserts causation.
Levels: a fact · b strong · c suggestive · d plausible · e hypothesis · g unknown.

## 1. What is known (a)
- Inorganic arsenic is an IARC **Group 1** human carcinogen — with *sufficient* human evidence for
  **bladder, lung, skin** (epithelial), and an *established* **hepatic angiosarcoma** (a sarcoma).
- Ewing sarcoma is defined by a **t(11;22) EWSR1::FLI1** (FET::ETS) fusion; cell of origin is a
  developmentally restricted **mesenchymal stem/progenitor cell** (embryonic-stage most permissive).
- Arsenic is genotoxic and **suppresses homologous-recombination repair** (not NHEJ), a state
  *associated with* chromosomal translocations and genomic instability.

## 2. What is suspected / plausible (c–d)
- A **plausible but unproven mechanistic bridge**: arsenic-driven demotion of high-fidelity repair +
  genomic instability could, in principle, favor formation or persistence of a translocation such as
  EWSR1::FLI1 — **never tested at the EWSR1 loci or in Ewing cells of origin.**
- Ewing has a **real, replicated epidemiological association with farm/agricultural/pesticide
  exposure** (maternal pesticide handling; paternal agricultural work, RR up to ~6). Historical
  arsenicals were agricultural pesticides — but those studies **did not isolate arsenic.**

## 3. What is not known (g)
- Whether environmentally relevant iAs increases EWSR1-region DSBs, mis-repair, or selects
  fusion-positive progenitors. Whether Ewing tumors carry any arsenic molecular signature. Whether
  any Ladera case had measurable historical arsenic exposure preceding diagnosis.

## 4. Evidence directly connecting arsenic to each reported cancer
See 06_other_cancers matrix. Summary: **bladder/lung/skin established; kidney/liver/prostate
suggestive; hepatic angiosarcoma established; STS limited/occupational; osteosarcoma & Ewing — no
direct evidence.**

## 5. Tumor tests that could strengthen or weaken the hypothesis (do not initiate — design for review)
- Blinded molecular analysis of Ewing FFPE: WGS/long-read (structural variants), RNA-seq, methylation
  array; **metallomics (tumor arsenic)**; test against any validated arsenic mutational/methylation
  signature. State clearly when a signature is non-specific.

## 6. Exposure biomarkers still recoverable (see 10_ + preservation checklist)
- **Deciduous teeth** (weekly-resolution prenatal→early-childhood metals, LA-ICP-MS; As feasible),
  **infant/adult toenails & hair** (validated As biomarkers), **archived newborn dried blood spots**
  (state-banked), **FFPE tumor arsenic**. These can reconstruct exposure *years before diagnosis* —
  **secure before disposal.**

## 7. Studies physicians should design (priority)
1. Blinded Ewing tumor molecular + metallomic study (cases vs sporadic controls).
2. Biomarker exposure reconstruction (teeth/nails/blood-spots) in cases + matched comparisons.
3. Mechanistic: env-relevant iAs in embryonic/pediatric mesenchymal progenitors → EWSR1 DSB/fusion/
   repair/selection assays (see 16_proposed_experiments).
4. CDC/ATSDR-compliant cluster evaluation with verified (not social-media) case counts.

## 8. Specialists / labs needed
Pediatric oncologist (Ewing), molecular pathologist, sarcoma cytogeneticist, environmental
toxicologist (arsenic speciation/bioavailability), exposure scientist (dental/nail biomarkers,
LA-ICP-MS), environmental epidemiologist, biostatistician (rare-event/spatial), CA Cancer Registry.

## 9. What would falsify or materially weaken the hypothesis
- No measurable historical arsenic exposure in affected individuals (teeth/nails/blood-spots null).
- Soil at documented dip ground shows no low-lead arsenic-trioxide signature above background.
- Ewing tumors show no arsenic-associated signature and typical sporadic genomics.
- Cases dominated by known germline predisposition / prior radiotherapy.
- Rigorous cluster analysis shows incidence within expected range for the true denominator.
> Any of these would not "disprove arsenic universally," but would remove this community's case for it.

**Counter-evidence to weigh (07_):** As2O3 *treats* the translocation leukemia APL; EWS-FLI1 alone
does not transform normal cells (cooperating events needed); Ewing has a strong germline/ancestry
component; arsenic's proven cancers are epithelial.
