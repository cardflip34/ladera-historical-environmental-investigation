# PROPOSED CRITICAL EXPERIMENTS (Workstream 14) — design only; execute only after GATE B

**Date:** 2026-07-23 · single-reviewer. Ranked by evidentiary value / feasibility. **No experiment is
initiated by this engine.** Environmentally relevant doses only — do not substitute unrealistic
high-dose work. Levels of current evidence they address in brackets.

## Tier 1 — resolve the local exposure predicate (fastest, cheapest, decisive-ish)
**E1. Authorized speciated soil sampling at priority grounds** [source/pathway: d→測]
- *Hypothesis:* a low-lead arsenic-trioxide ("dip") signature exists above background at the documented/
  probable dip ground (Bell Canyon/Starr Ranch vicinity) and/or child-contact areas.
- *Design:* 0–6 in child-contact horizon + depth profiles at tank/drip-pad analogs; **total +
  bioaccessible As, lead, speciation, organochlorines**; background controls outside cattle-use area.
- *Endpoints:* As concentration; **As:Pb ratio + speciation** (dip vs orchard); bioaccessibility.
- *Value:* high — converts a paper hazard to measured presence/absence. *Ethics:* landowner + agency
  permission (Audubon for Starr Ranch); chain of custody. *Limit:* mass grading may have diluted signal.

## Tier 2 — reconstruct individual historical exposure (temporality + gradient)
**E2. Biomarker exposure reconstruction** [temporality: g→測]
- Deciduous-tooth dentine LA-ICP-MS (weekly-resolution prenatal→early-childhood As), infant/adult
  toenails, archived newborn dried blood-spot metals/methylation, in affected vs matched comparison.
- *Endpoints:* reconstructed As timeline vs etiologic window; the 255-CpG methylation signature.
- *Value:* high; the only route to *exposure-before-disease*. *Ethics:* IRB + specimen consent; PHI.

## Tier 2 — tumor molecular (feasibility-limited; see 11_)
**E3. Blinded Ewing FFPE metallomics + methylation + EWSR1-breakpoint analysis**
- *Endpoints:* tumor arsenic; arsenic methylation signature; breakpoint microhomology/repair
  fingerprint; (exploratory) T>G fraction — with the **low-mutation-burden caveat**.
- *Value:* medium (non-specific signatures; sparse mutations). *Ethics:* finite blocks; legal hold.

## Tier 3 — the mechanistic crux (arsenic → EWSR1)
**E4. EWSR1 double-strand-break induction** — env-relevant iAs in human embryonic/pediatric mesenchymal
progenitors; measure DSBs (γH2AX, breakpoint PCR) at/around EWSR1. [plausibility d→test]
**E5. Repair-fidelity assay** — does iAs bias repair of an induced EWSR1-region break toward
translocation (HR-suppression readout, given As lowers HR not NHEJ)?
**E6. Fusion-formation / selection assay** — does iAs increase de-novo EWSR1::FLI1 formation, or select/
expand cells already carrying it? (recall EWS-FLI1 alone needs cooperating events)
- *Value:* highest for *general causation mechanism*; *Feasibility:* hard; specialized labs.
- *Controls:* vehicle; positive genotoxin; APL counter-model (arsenic kills fusion cells) to bound effect direction.

## Tier 3 — population
**E7. Preregistered CDC/ATSDR epidemiology** (Workstream 10) with verified CCR counts, ancestry
standardization, SaTScan + Bayesian; **E8. gene–environment** study of arsenic-metabolism variants
(AS3MT) in cases; **E9. comparison population** near a *documented* dip-contaminated site.

## Prioritization logic
Do **E1 + E2** first (cheapest, resolve exposure predicate + temporality). E4–E6 answer the mechanism
but are slow. E7 needs authorized registry data. Every experiment: pre-specified endpoints, blinding
where possible, replication, confounder control (13_), ethics + chain of custody.

## Falsification value (ties to TOP_15)
Null E1 (no dip signature) + null E2 (no reconstructed exposure) would substantially weaken the
community-specific hypothesis without needing the hard mechanism work.
