## Objective
Determine whether a **low-lead arsenic-trioxide ("cattle-dip") signature** exists above local background
at documented/probable dip grounds and at child-contact areas — and **distinguish it from the orchard
lead-arsenate signature**. Resolve exposure link (ii) from *plausible* toward *measured*.

## Hypotheses tested (from TOP_20)
H1 dip signature above background at the documented dip ground; H2 dip signature in child-contact areas;
plus confounder test (orchard lead-arsenate; organochlorines).

## Analyte suite (the discriminator is the whole point)
| Analyte | Method (indicative) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Total arsenic** | EPA 6010/6020 (ICP-MS) | concentration vs background (~≤12 mg/kg SoCal upper bound) |
| **Total lead** | EPA 6010/6020 | **As:Pb ratio** — dip (low-Pb) vs orchard (As+Pb) |
| **Arsenic speciation** (As(III)/As(V)) | IC-ICP-MS / XANES | source + toxicity form |
| **Bioaccessible arsenic** | in-vitro bioaccessibility (e.g., SBRC/UBM) | hazard→dose realism (not total alone) |
| **Organochlorines** (DDT/DDE/DDD, chlordane) | EPA 8081 | co-contaminant / alternative source (13_) |
| **Iron/Mn oxides, pH, OM, texture** | standard soil | As binding/mobility context |

## Interpretation rules (pre-specified)
- **Dip signature:** arsenic **>> background with low/absent lead** (low As:Pb) → supports a dip source.
- **Orchard signature:** arsenic **elevated with co-elevated lead** (lead-arsenate) → points to orchard,
  **not** the dip (confounder wins).
- **Organochlorine co-elevation** → agricultural source mixture; weigh in 13_.
- **Null (≤ background):** does not fully clear the site (grading may have diluted/removed surface
  residue) — interpret with the site-disturbance history; still substantially weakens H1/H2.
- Report totals **and** bioaccessible fractions; never infer dose from total alone.

## Screening references (context, not verdicts)
SoCal ambient arsenic mean ~1.5 mg/kg, upper bound ~12 mg/kg (school-PEA dataset); WA former-orchard
cleanup >20 ppm; former dip sites elsewhere reach 34–2,941 mg/kg near tank/drip-pad. Use local
background from the control points, not a fixed number.
