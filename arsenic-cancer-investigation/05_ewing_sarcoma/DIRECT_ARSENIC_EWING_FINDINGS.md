# DIRECT ARSENIC–EWING SARCOMA FINDINGS (Priority 1)

**Search date:** 2026-07-23 · **Reviewer:** research-engine (single-reviewer; flag for dual review)
**Bottom line up front:** No study directly linking inorganic arsenic exposure to Ewing sarcoma was
located. This is a **documented absence of research**, not evidence of no relationship. Arsenic *is*
an established human carcinogen, but for **different tissue types** (epithelial: bladder, lung, skin),
not bone/mesenchymal sarcomas. A mechanistic bridge exists only at the level of **general
genotoxicity / DNA-repair inhibition (biological plausibility, level d)** and has **not** been
connected to the specific *EWSR1* translocation that defines Ewing sarcoma.

Evidence levels used: a established fact · b strong · c suggestive · d biological plausibility ·
e hypothesis · f inference · g unknown.

---

## Finding 1 — No direct arsenic–Ewing literature located · level (a) for the *absence*
A targeted search (see SEARCH_LOG entry SL-2026-07-23-01) returned **zero** studies examining
inorganic arsenic exposure as a risk factor for Ewing sarcoma. Reviews and GWAS of Ewing etiology
describe **"no established environmental risk factors"**; the disease is defined by *FET::ETS*
fusions (*EWSR1::FLI1* in >85%). Arsenic is not discussed as a candidate exposure.
- Grossmann/GWAS susceptibility loci: PMC6085378; 2025 GWAS meta-analysis PMC12919121.
- NCI Ewing Sarcoma Treatment (PDQ), health-professional version — etiology section.
> **Do not convert this absence into "arsenic does not cause Ewing sarcoma."** It means the specific
> question has never been studied. It defines a research gap (see 20_research_gaps).

## Finding 2 — Arsenic is a Group 1 carcinogen, but for epithelial sites, not sarcoma · level (a)
IARC classifies inorganic arsenic as **Group 1 (carcinogenic to humans)**. Sites with **sufficient
human evidence: urinary bladder, lung, skin** (non-melanoma). These are **carcinomas (epithelial)**.
**Bone sarcoma / Ewing (mesenchymal) is not among the established arsenic cancer sites.**
- IARC Monograph Vol. 100C, "Arsenic and Arsenic Compounds" — NCBI Bookshelf NBK304380; overall
  evaluation NBK533643; tumour-site concordance NBK570318.
- **Specificity caveat (level a):** established evidence for arsenic concerns a different cell
  lineage than Ewing. Evidence for one tumor type does **not** transfer to another (governance rule).

## Finding 3 — A general genotoxic bridge exists but is not Ewing-specific · level (d)
Arsenic carcinogenesis mechanisms (well characterized for its established sites) include: oxidative
DNA damage (ROS), **inhibition of DNA repair** (base-excision repair; binding zinc-finger repair
proteins such as PARP), chromosomal aberrations, genomic instability, cell-cycle disruption, and
epigenetic dysregulation.
- Review: Arsenic carcinogenesis — epidemiology, metabolism, genotoxicity, epigenetics. PubMed
  **30223072** (Sci Total/Regul Toxicol Pharmacol, 2018).
- Arsenic disruption of DNA-damage responses: PMC4693233 (Biomolecules 2015).
- Arsenic co-carcinogenesis / inhibition of DNA repair + zinc-finger proteins: ScienceDirect
  S1044579X21001371.
> **The bridge is plausible but non-specific.** Inhibited repair + genomic instability is a
> *mechanism by which translocations can arise/persist in general*; **no source shows arsenic
> produces or selects the *EWSR1* fusion.** Do not equate general DNA damage with proof of an
> EWSR1-fusion mechanism (governance rule). This is the central question for WORKSTREAM 4.

## What this defines for the next steps
1. **WORKSTREAM 4 mechanistic bridge** is the pivotal unknown: can environmentally relevant iAs
   increase *EWSR1*-region double-strand breaks, impair their repair, or select fusion-positive
   progenitors? (See 16_proposed_experiments — critical experiments 12–15.)
2. **Molecular-pathological epidemiology:** is there a validated *arsenic mutational/methylation
   signature*, and could it be tested (blinded) against Ewing tumors? (WORKSTREAM 9.)
3. **Specificity discipline:** every downstream claim must respect that arsenic's proven cancers are
   epithelial, and that Ewing has a single defining genetic lesion of unknown environmental trigger.
