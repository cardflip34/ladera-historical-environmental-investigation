# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY — Arsenic & the reported cancers (foundation established 2026-07-23)

## Where this stands
The evidence-repository **framework is built and the Priority-1 question has a first, honest,
sourced answer.** This is the start of a large multi-session scientific build, not a finished
review. Nothing here asserts causation.

## The single most important finding so far (Priority 1)
- **No study links inorganic arsenic to Ewing sarcoma.** Ewing has *no established environmental
  risk factors* and is defined by the *EWSR1::FLI1* fusion. → *documented gap, not a negative result.*
- **Arsenic is a Group 1 human carcinogen — but for bladder, lung, and skin (epithelial cancers),
  not bone/mesenchymal sarcoma.** Evidence for one tumor type does not transfer to another.
- A **general genotoxic bridge** (oxidative DNA damage, DNA-repair inhibition, genomic instability)
  is biologically plausible but **has never been connected to the specific EWSR1 fusion.**
- **Full detail + citations:** `05_ewing_sarcoma/DIRECT_ARSENIC_EWING_FINDINGS.md`.

## The pivotal unknown (drives everything downstream)
Could environmentally relevant inorganic arsenic **increase EWSR1-region DNA breaks, impair their
repair, or select fusion-positive progenitor cells?** No one has tested this. It is the crux of
WORKSTREAM 4 and the proposed critical experiments.
