# PROJECT SCOPE — Arsenic & the Ladera Ranch reported cancers

**Status:** foundation established 2026-07-23. This is a physician-directed, attorney-supervised
evidence-repository build. It follows the completed historical investigation (mandated 1907–1912
arsenical cattle dipping in South Orange County) and does **not** re-litigate that history except to
verify a medical or exposure claim.

## Core research question
Could historical **inorganic arsenic** exposure plausibly cause, initiate, promote, accelerate, or
interact with genetic susceptibility in the development of **Ewing sarcoma** and the other confirmed
rare cancers reported among Ladera Ranch residents?

"Arsenic and cancer" is **not** sufficient. The build focuses on cancer specificity, pediatric
exposure, tissue of origin, molecular mechanism, exposure timing, and dose.

## What this repository is / is not
- **Is:** a transparent, reproducible, source-graded evidence base so independent physicians,
  epidemiologists, toxicologists, molecular biologists, environmental scientists, and attorneys can
  judge exactly what is established, plausible, unresolved, or unsupported.
- **Is not:** an advocacy document, a causation claim, or a legal conclusion.

## Evidence grading (applied to every substantive statement)
`a. established fact · b. strong evidence · c. suggestive evidence · d. biological plausibility ·
e. hypothesis · f. inference · g. unknown`

## Non-negotiable rules
- Follow the evidence; never confirm a predetermined conclusion.
- Do not state arsenic causes Ewing sarcoma unless a source states that exact conclusion.
- Every substantive claim carries an inline citation + a full source record (see DATABASE_SCHEMA).
- Prefer primary research, government reports, systematic reviews, authoritative monographs.
- **Never fabricate** a citation, DOI, PMID, quotation, statistic, author, study, or conclusion.
- A documented **absence** of research is a finding — never converted into evidence of no effect.
- Capture contradictory/negative evidence with equal diligence.
- Flag retractions, expressions of concern, preprints, and predatory journals.
- **PHI stays private** (folder 04, gitignored). Public outputs use aggregation/masking only.
- Do not contact families, witnesses, agencies, researchers, or defendants. No legal conclusions.
- Preserve originals + hashes; never overwrite source files.

## Priority order (active)
1. Direct arsenic–Ewing literature, incl. negative findings and gaps. *(in progress)*
2. Arsenic mechanisms that could create structural DNA damage / gene fusions / defective repair /
   selection of fusion-positive cells.
3. Ewing etiology, environmental epidemiology, developmental biology.
4. Disease-specific arsenic evidence for every other verified cancer.
5. Historical-exposure biomarkers + tumor-testing opportunities at risk of loss.
6. Experiments testing EWSR1 breakage / fusion / repair / selection under environmentally relevant As.
7. CDC/ATSDR-compliant epidemiological + spatial design.

## Reproducibility standard
Independent experts must be able to re-run every search (SEARCH_LOG.md), inspect every source
(02_source_library + hashes), and challenge every assumption (19_claims_audit). The output is judged
by reproducibility and defensibility, not persuasiveness.
