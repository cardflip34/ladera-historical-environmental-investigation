# CONTRADICTORY / WEAKENING EVIDENCE (weighted equally with supportive)

Consolidated so the case against the arsenic->Ewing hypothesis is as visible as the case for it.

1. **No direct study** links arsenic to Ewing (05_). Absence of research, but also absence of support.
2. **Arsenic's dominant MOA is cytotoxicity/regenerative proliferation / oxidative stress (often
   non-genotoxic)** - not translocation-formation (14_; S021,S022). The mechanism the hypothesis needs
   is a *minority* route.
3. **Tissue-tropism mismatch:** arsenic's established cancers are **epithelial** (bladder/lung/skin);
   Ewing is **mesenchymal bone** (06_).
4. **Counter-analogy:** arsenic trioxide **cures** APL, a fusion-driven leukemia - arsenic can destroy
   translocation-cancer cells, not only cause them (05_,07_).
5. **EWS-FLI1 alone does not transform normal human cells;** cooperating events are required, and the
   fusion itself impairs DNA repair - the rate-limiting step may not be break-formation (07_,S006).
6. **Strong germline/ancestry component:** GWAS loci; ~9x higher in white than Black individuals -
   points to heritable drivers and demands ancestry standardization (10_,S014,S017).
7. **Ewing's very low mutation burden (0.15/Mb)** limits power to detect an arsenic mutational
   signature in the tumor (09_,S25010205).
8. **The replicated environmental Ewing signal is "pesticide/farm/parental-occupation," not arsenic;**
   arsenic was never isolated, and organochlorines co-occur (12_,13_).
9. **Local exposure unmeasured:** dip-site arsenic persistence is shown *elsewhere*; no local soil,
   dust, or biomarker data exist (08_,14_).
10. **Small-number/Texas-Sharpshooter risk** in any community cluster claim (10_).

**Net:** these do not disprove the hypothesis, but they make **causation currently unsupported** and
define why measurement (not assertion) is the only defensible path.

11. **Directional counter-signal (native-db, reproducible):** the entire published arsenic-Ewing
    literature is **arsenic trioxide as a *treatment*** (it kills Ewing cells, like it treats APL) -
    `arsenic AND EWSR1` = 0, `arsenic AND EWS-FLI1` = 0 in PubMed. Zero etiologic studies exist.
