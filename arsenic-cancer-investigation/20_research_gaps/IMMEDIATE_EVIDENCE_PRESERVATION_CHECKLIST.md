# IMMEDIATE EVIDENCE-PRESERVATION CHECKLIST (time-critical)

**Why this is first:** several evidence streams that could reconstruct exposure *years before
diagnosis* or fix the environmental record are **destroyable or degrade with time**. Preserving them
does not assume causation — it protects the ability to test the question either way. Physician/legal
teams should secure chain of custody; **this project does not collect specimens or contact families.**

Level key: a fact · b strong · c suggestive · d plausible · e hypothesis · g unknown.

## B. Environmental samples — HIGH urgency (irreversible if disturbed)
- **Soil at documented/probable dip ground (Bell Canyon / Starr Ranch vicinity) and school/park play
  areas — BEFORE any grading, remediation, or development disturbs it.** Test total + bioaccessible
  arsenic, **with lead**, plus **speciation** (to separate dip-signature arsenic trioxide from
  orchard lead-arsenate). Once soil is moved/capped, the depth profile and signature are lost.
- **Any predevelopment / Phase-I era soil archives or boring logs** for Ladera and Coto tracts.
- Chain-of-custody sampling only; authorized + landowner-permitted (Audubon for Starr Ranch).

## C. Documentary/records — MEDIUM urgency (see historical investigation's archive list)

  RMV archive); County Veterinarian orders + Supervisors minutes (OC Archives); State Vet files (CA
  State Archives). These could name a vat location. Private archives can deaccession.

## D. Registry/medical (private, folder 04) — MEDIUM urgency
