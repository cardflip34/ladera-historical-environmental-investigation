## How this constrains the arsenic case
- The **single most important confounder is the orchard lead-arsenate signature** — many local/near
  sites tested positive for arsenic *with lead* (the orchard kind). **Only low-lead speciation
  distinguishes a dip source from an orchard source.**
- The **replicated environmental Ewing signal is "pesticide/farm/parental-occupation," not arsenic** —
  arsenic has never been isolated within it. A defensible analysis must **model organochlorines and
  parental occupation** before attributing to arsenic.
- **Ancestry/germline** must be adjusted (Workstream 10) or an apparent excess in an affluent,
  higher-white community is partly expected baseline.

## Rule
Do not let alternatives dilute the arsenic investigation — but **no arsenic attribution is defensible
until these are measured and excluded/adjusted** at the individual and area level.
