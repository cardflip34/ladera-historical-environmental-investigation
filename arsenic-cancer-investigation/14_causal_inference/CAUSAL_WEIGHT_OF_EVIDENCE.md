# CAUSAL WEIGHT OF EVIDENCE (Workstream 12)

**Date:** 2026-07-23 · single-reviewer (flag dual review) · **hypothesis-neutral.** Applies multiple
frameworks (Bradford Hill, source–pathway–receptor, mode-of-action) to the question *"did historical
inorganic arsenic cause Ewing sarcoma in this community?"* — **not** as a checklist that yields a
verdict, but to show which links have evidence and which do not. Levels: a fact · b strong ·
c suggestive · d plausible · e hypothesis · f inference · g unknown.

## Headline
On the **specific** question (arsenic → Ewing), the weight of evidence is currently **weak /
unestablished**: most Bradford-Hill considerations are **unmet or unstudied**, plausibility is
**moderate but mechanistically uncertain**, and there is real **incoherence** (tissue tropism +
dominant arsenic mode of action). The **environmental predicate** (arsenic can persist at high levels
at former dip grounds) is comparatively well-supported by analog sites.

## Mode-of-action check (why plausibility is tempered)
Arsenic's **accepted MOA for its established cancers** centers on **oxidative stress + cytotoxicity
with regenerative proliferation** (often described as *non-genotoxic* carcinogenesis), plus epigenetic
and repair effects. That MOA does **not** map cleanly onto "generate a specific EWSR1 translocation in
a mesenchymal progenitor." The genotoxic/HR-suppression thread (Bridge A) is a *minority* mechanistic
route. **Honest read: the mechanism most responsible for arsenic's proven cancers is not the mechanism
the Ewing hypothesis requires** — a genuine weakness to state plainly.

## Triangulation across independent streams
- Mechanistic (07_): plausible-not-proven.
- Epidemiologic (12_): Ewing–pesticide signal real but not arsenic-resolved; local cluster unverified.
- Exposure/environmental (below, 09_): dip-site arsenic persistence well-analogized; local unmeasured.
- Molecular (09_): no arsenic signature tested against Ewing.
→ The streams **converge on plausibility and on a shared set of decisive, unrun tests**, not on
demonstrated causation.

## What would move the needle (feeds 16_)
Establish **temporality + gradient** via biomarker exposure reconstruction; **experiment** via
EWSR1-break/fusion assays under environmentally relevant arsenic; **coherence** via local soil
speciation (dip signature vs orchard); **molecular** via blinded tumor signature testing.

## General vs specific causation
- **General:** arsenic → Ewing is **not** established (unstudied); arsenic → *a* human sarcoma is
  established (angiosarcoma).
- **Specific:** unestablished; requires local arsenic + reconstructed individual exposure in the
  etiologic window + an accepted mechanism/biomarker link.
