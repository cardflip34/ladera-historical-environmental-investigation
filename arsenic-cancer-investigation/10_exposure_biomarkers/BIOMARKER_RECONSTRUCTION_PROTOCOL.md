# BIOMARKER EXPOSURE-RECONSTRUCTION PROTOCOL — E2 (DRAFT, gated)

**Status:** DRAFT for expert/IRB review. **Not executed by this engine.** Requires Ethics/IRB approval,
individual consent, and chain of custody (GATE B). PHI stays in folder 04. Time-sensitive: teeth, nails,
and archived newborn blood-spot cards are discarded/degrade on schedules. Levels: a fact · b strong ·
d plausible · g unknown.

## Objective
Reconstruct **individual historical inorganic-arsenic exposure**, especially the **prenatal/early-life
etiologic window** relevant to Ewing, in affected individuals vs matched comparisons — to test
**temporality** and **dose-gradient** (the two Bradford-Hill elements currently at level g).

## Rationale (validated methods)
- **Deciduous ("baby") teeth:** dentine grows in incremental layers; **LA-ICP-MS** reconstructs metal
  exposure at ~weekly resolution from **2nd trimester through early childhood** (validated Pb/Mn/Ba/Sr;
  As feasible). PMID capture: S011.
- **Toenails / fingernails:** validated **in-utero and long-term arsenic** biomarker (S012; 19280039).
- **Archived newborn dried blood-spot cards:** state-banked at birth; enable **methylation** (255-CpG
  arsenic signature, S028) and metal analysis; used in childhood-cancer discovery (S013).
- **Hair:** recent months only; high external-contamination risk → supportive, not primary.

## Design
- **Groups (blinded to lab):** (i) affected individuals; (ii) unaffected community comparisons matched
  on age/sex/ancestry/residence-duration; (iii) external comparisons from a non-exposed area.
- **Specimens per participant (as available/consented):** shed deciduous teeth; toe/fingernail
  clippings; access to the archived newborn blood-spot card (state program, legal pathway).
- **Timeline reconstruction:** map dentine/nail As to calendar age; align to residence/school history
  (private, 04_) and the **etiologic window** (prenatal/early-life).

## Analytical methods (indicative; lab-certified, blinded)
| Specimen | Method | Readout |
|---|---|---|
| Tooth dentine | LA-ICP-MS micro-spatial + histology | weekly As (and Pb) trajectory, 2nd-trimester→early childhood |
| Nails | trace-metal ICP-MS (acid digest) | integrated As over months; As species where feasible |
| Blood-spot card | metal ICP-MS + EPIC methylation | birth As; 255-CpG arsenic exposure signature |
| (all) | run **lead alongside arsenic** | separate dip vs orchard exposure context |

## Endpoints (pre-specified)
Primary: reconstructed As concentration/timeline in the etiologic window, affected vs comparison.
Secondary: presence of the arsenic methylation signature; As:Pb pattern; concordance across specimens.

## QA/QC, ethics, custody
Field/reagent blanks, reference materials, duplicate analyses; **blinded** case/control labels until
lock; documented chain of custody; IRB + individual consent; **destructive testing minimized** (teeth/
blood spots are finite — micro-sampling; retain remainder). No specimen handled by this engine.

## Interpretation limits (state up front)
- A biomarker **does not prove causation** of a specific tumor; it establishes/【refutes】exposure and
  timing. **Elevated + well-timed exposure strengthens; a null weakens** the community-specific case.
- Contamination and metabolism (AS3MT genotype) confound — measure and adjust.
- Small n → report individual trajectories + exact CIs; avoid over-pooling.

## Sequence & pairing
Run **with E1 (soil)**: soil says *was arsenic there*; biomarkers say *did this person carry it, when*.
Together they are the strongest, cheapest early read on links (ii)–(v) before the hard mechanism work.

## Falsification value (F2/F3)
Null reconstructed exposure across affected individuals would substantially weaken the hypothesis
regardless of mechanism.
