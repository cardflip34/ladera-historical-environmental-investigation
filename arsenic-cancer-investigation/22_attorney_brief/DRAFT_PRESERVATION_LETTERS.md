# DRAFT PRESERVATION & RECORDS-REQUEST TEMPLATES (for the legal team; NOT to be sent by this engine)

**Status:** DRAFTS for the supervising attorney to review, adapt, and — at their discretion — send.
**This engine does not contact any person, agency, custodian, or family, and draws no legal
conclusions.** Bracketed `[…]` fields are placeholders. Purpose: stop loss of time-sensitive evidence
(Phase 3), pending GATE B. Nothing herein is legal advice.

## Template A — Litigation/evidence hold: archived newborn dried blood-spot cards
To: [State newborn-screening program / biobank custodian]
Re: Preservation request — dried blood-spot specimens
"[Firm/Physician] requests that the [program] **preserve and not destroy or deaccession** the dried
blood-spot specimens and associated records for [individuals/cohort — identified separately under
appropriate authorization], which may be material to an ongoing health investigation. We ask that
routine destruction schedules be suspended for these specimens pending further request, and that you
confirm current retention status and the process for authorized research access. [Consent/authority
basis]. Please preserve chain of custody."

Re: Preservation of pathology specimens
"Please **preserve and do not exhaust, discard, or return** the formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded
blocks, slides, and associated pathology records for [patient(s), per authorization], including any

## Template C — Family keepsake retention (routed via the family's own clinician/counsel)
Note for the team: **do not contact families directly through this project.** Via their own
clinicians/counsel, provide guidance to **retain** shed baby teeth, nail clippings, and similar
keepsakes (do not discard), as these may enable exposure reconstruction. No collection occurs without
IRB approval + consent.

and ranch photograph collections. Please advise on access procedures and confirm these materials are
