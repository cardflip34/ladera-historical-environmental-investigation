# ATTORNEY SCIENCE BRIEF (v0.1, 2026-07-23)

Scientific, not argumentative. **Preliminary, single-reviewer, hypothesis-neutral. No legal
conclusions.** Distinguishes general vs specific causation. Levels: a fact · b strong · c suggestive
· d plausible · e hypothesis · g unknown.

## 1. Which links in the causal chain have evidence, and which are missing
Chain: **(i) arsenic used at the parcel → (ii) arsenic released/retained in soil → (iii) soil
redistributed to child-contact areas → (iv) residents exposed → (v) exposure preceded diagnosis →
(vi) arsenic capable of causing the specific cancer → (vii) it did in these individuals.**

| Link | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| i Arsenic used at the parcel | **g / needs location-specific evidence** | statewide mandate ≠ parcel proof; 1908 Joplin dip documented on Trabuco/Bell Canyon ground (historical investigation) |
| ii Release/retention in soil | **d** (arsenic persists; element, no decay) | not yet measured at these parcels |
| iii Redistribution by grading | **e/g** | plausible via mass grading; unmeasured |
| iv Resident exposure | **g** | no biomarker data yet |
| v Exposure preceded diagnosis | **g** | reconstructable via teeth/nails/blood-spots (10_) |
| vi General causation, As → this cancer | **bladder/lung/skin a; hepatic angiosarcoma a; Ewing g** | arsenic can cause *a* sarcoma; **no evidence for Ewing/bone** |
| vii Specific causation | **g** | not established |

## 2. Which assertions are presently too strong (do not advance as fact)
- "Arsenic causes Ewing sarcoma." — **unsupported** (no direct study).
- "Dip vats existed within the development footprint." — **needs location-specific evidence** (Ladera
  footprint: none documented; Bell Canyon/Coto: 1908 dip documented nearby).
- "Contaminated soil was redistributed and residents were exposed." — **unverified** (no soil/biomarker data).
- "Cancer incidence is statistically elevated." — **requires CDC/ATSDR analysis with verified counts**
  (not social-media tallies). (See 12_epidemiology.)

## 3. General vs specific causation (Daubert-relevant)
- **General causation** (can arsenic cause the disease type): **established for bladder/lung/skin and
  hepatic angiosarcoma; NOT established for Ewing/bone sarcoma** — currently a research gap, not proof
  either way.
- **Specific causation** (did it cause these plaintiffs' cancers): **unestablished**; would require
  documented parcel arsenic, reconstructed individual exposure preceding the etiologic window, and a
  scientifically accepted mechanism/biomarker linking the two.

## 4. Records/datasets that would resolve major uncertainties
Location-specific: ranch ledgers/receipts, County Vet orders, State Vet files (archive list).
Environmental: authorized soil sampling (total+bioaccessible As, **lead**, **speciation**) at priority
grounds. Medical: pathology + EWSR1 status, residence/school histories (private). Denominators: CA
Cancer Registry, SEER.

## 5. Evidence requiring formal chain of custody
Soil samples; biological specimens (teeth, nails, blood-spot cards, FFPE blocks); original archival
documents. **Sampling should occur before any soil disturbance/remediation** (link ii/iii degrade).

## 6. Evidence that may disappear with time (see IMMEDIATE_EVIDENCE_PRESERVATION_CHECKLIST)
Baby teeth, nails, **archived newborn blood-spot cards** (retention schedules), **FFPE blocks**
(hospital limits), predevelopment soil/boring archives, private ranch records. **Legal holds are
time-sensitive.**

## 7. Alternative causes / confounders that must be addressed (15_)
Lead-arsenate (orchard) vs arsenic-trioxide (dip) signatures; organochlorines (DDT/chlordane);
other metals; germline Ewing predisposition; ancestry; chance aggregation; ascertainment/
media-recruitment bias; residential mobility; other environmental sources.

## 8. Potential experts by issue
Molecular pathology/cytogenetics; arsenic toxicology + speciation; exposure science (dental/nail
biomarkers); environmental epidemiology; biostatistics (rare events/spatial); geomorphology/soil
(redistribution); pediatric oncology.

## 9. Bottom line for counsel (scientific)
The **historical/ environmental** predicate is partially documented; the **medical-causation**
predicate for Ewing is **currently unproven and largely unstudied**. The defensible posture is to
**preserve evidence and commission the specific studies** (soil signature, individual biomarker
reconstruction, blinded tumor molecular analysis, compliant cluster evaluation) that would move each
link from *plausible/unknown* toward *established or excluded* — not to assert causation now.
