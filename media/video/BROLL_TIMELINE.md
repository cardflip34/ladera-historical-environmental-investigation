## Shot-treatment legend

- **ME FULL** = camera on Andy, full frame, no image. Used for the human/emotional
  and integrity moments so the connection lands.
- **B-ROLL + PiP** = the image fills the frame, Andy stays in a small box, lower-right.
  Used for every evidence/history moment.
- **SPLIT/PAN** = slow ken-burns push or a then/now wipe on a single image.

The film breathes: it moves **out to full-screen Andy** for the personal beats, and
**in to B-roll with Andy in the corner** whenever there is something to show.
