Corrected 20 September 2026 from the held gubernatorial proclamations (California State
Library scans, A1).

| Date | Governor | Orange County extent |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Mar 1910 | Gillett | **entire county** |
| 16 Oct 1911 | Johnson | **entire county** |
| 7 Mar 1912 | Johnson | **PARTIAL** — retained the south and west |
| 1 Mar 1915 | Johnson | Orange named as still having quarantined areas |

**The 7 March 1912 proclamation did not end the quarantine.** It released the northern
county and kept the southern portion, bounded by a line running Newport Beach → the
Newport-Tustin road → the Santa Ana-Trabuco road → the Aliso Canyon road → **the north
line of Township 6 South, Range 7 West** → east to the county line.

**T6S R7W is the township containing Ladera Ranch and the former Trabuco / Mission Viejo
rancho land.** The O'Neill holdings sat inside the retained quarantine area.

The 1 March 1915 proclamation names Orange County in its preamble, so some area was still
quarantined then. **The copy held is missing page 2**, which carries the area description,
so the 1915 extent is not established — obtain a complete copy.

**Why:** an earlier note in this project said the OC quarantine ended March 1912. That is
wrong and would understate the exposure window by at least three years.

**How to apply:** the compulsory arsenical dipping window for this land is 1910 through at
least 1915, not 1907–1912. Related: [[joplin-patent-geography-correction]],
[[ladera-eir555-and-monitoring-mandate]].
