## Hard rules that travel with every request in this register

1. **Ask for a fee estimate before any chargeable work.** Every drafted letter does. Keep it.
2. **Request rolling production.** Index records first, so follow-ups can be narrowed to what
   actually exists.
3. **Never request individual health, student, or member information.** Several letters say so
   explicitly and instruct the custodian to redact. That is deliberate.
4. **Nothing here is a certificate of occupancy** except item 01. Not a road acceptance, not a
   year-built value, not a school opening, not a notice of completion, not a first deed, not an
   aerial observation. Every draft carries a "Do not claim from the response" section enforcing this.
5. **Hypothesis-neutral phrasing.** No letter alleges that any product, agency, vendor, employer or
   association caused any harm, and none may be edited to do so.
6. **Preservation before production.** Where records may be destroyed (Tier 0), ask for preservation
   first — it is free for the custodian and irreversible if skipped.
