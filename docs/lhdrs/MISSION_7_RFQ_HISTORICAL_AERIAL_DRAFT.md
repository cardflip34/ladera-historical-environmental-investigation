## Why this request exists

Free open catalogs were swept first (2026-07-28). Result:
- Landsat covers 1999-2004 densely but at **30 m**, which cannot resolve rooftops or parcels.
- NAIP returns nothing for the AOI before 2005.
- The County aerial catalog is marked exhausted for development-era frames.

So high-resolution imagery of the 1999-2004 grading and construction period, if it exists, is most
likely held by commercial historical-aerial archives. This RFQ asks whether it exists and at what
cost, **before** any purchase commitment.

> I am looking for **high-resolution aerial imagery covering calendar years 1999 through 2004**, and

>
> **What I am asking:**
> 1. Which flight dates in 1999-2004 intersect this area?
> 2. For each: capture date, ground sample distance, colour or B&W, and whether it is

## Rules for whoever sends this

1. **Check licensing before purchasing, not after.** If imagery cannot be published or its derived
   analysis cannot be published, its value to this project is sharply reduced. Capture the licence
   text into the immutable source store (L0) with the imagery.
2. **Do not describe the project's hypotheses or health-related context in vendor correspondence.**
   The request is for imagery of a place and time. Keep it that way.
3. Get dates and GSD **before** paying. A frame that does not intersect the AOI, or is too coarse to
   resolve structures, does not advance Phase 2 or 4.
4. Log every response, including "no coverage," into the acquisition record. A vendor negative is a
   finding and closes a line of inquiry.
