## Before sending — one question decides the letter

**Is the requester a LARMAC member (a Ladera Ranch homeowner)?**

- **Yes → use Version A.** Cite Davis-Stirling. A member has an enforceable statutory right to
  inspect association records, and the association must respond within defined timeframes.
- **No → use Version B.** A purely voluntary request. Courteous, no legal claim, easy to decline.

Both versions lead with the preservation ask, because that is the part that matters most and costs
the association nothing.

---

## Version A — if the requester is a LARMAC member

> **Subject:** Member records inspection request — common-area landscape and pest-control records
>
> To the LARMAC Board and Managing Agent:
>
> I am a member of the Ladera Ranch Maintenance Corporation. Under Civil Code §§ 5200–5240 I request
> to inspect and obtain copies of the association records described below.
>
> **First, and most important — a preservation request.** Before anything else, I ask that the
> association **preserve, and instruct its vendors to preserve**, all records described below,
> including any scheduled for routine destruction. I am not alleging wrongdoing by the association
> or by any vendor. I am asking that historical records not be discarded while this research is
> under way. This costs the association nothing and is the single most valuable thing it can do.
>
> **Records requested**, for common areas, slopes, parks, trails, parkways and drainage corridors,
> from the earliest available date through the present:
>
> 1. **Landscape and pest-control service contracts and scopes of work**, including vendor names and
>    the periods each vendor served.
> 2. **Pesticide, herbicide, fungicide and rodenticide application records** — application logs,
>    service tickets, route sheets, or vendor reports showing **product name, EPA registration
>    number, active ingredient, date, quantity, and location applied**.
> 3. **Invoices and product purchase records** sufficient to identify products applied, where
>    application logs no longer exist.
> 4. **Board and committee minutes, agendas and staff reports** concerning landscape maintenance,
>    pest control, soil amendment, soil import, or turf and slope treatment.
> 5. **Soil testing, geotechnical or agronomic reports** commissioned by the association for common
>    areas, if any exist.
>
> **What I am not asking for.** No homeowner names, addresses, account numbers, delinquency,
> violation, or other individual member information. No personnel records. If a responsive document
> contains member-identifying information, please redact it — I do not want it.
>
> **Purpose.** Independent historical land-use research into what was applied to common ground in
> this community and when. It is hypothesis-neutral: it does not assume that any product caused any
> harm, and it makes no claim that the association or any vendor did anything improper.
>
> **Format.** Whatever is easiest — scans, photographs of paper files, spreadsheet exports. I am
> happy to inspect in person, to pay reasonable copying costs under § 5205, and to work to whatever
> schedule suits the office.
>
> Sincerely,
> [NAME] · [PROPERTY ADDRESS AS MEMBER] · [CONTACT] · [DATE]

## Version B — if the requester is not a member

> **Subject:** Voluntary request — historical common-area landscape and pest-control records
>
> To the LARMAC Board and Managing Agent:
>
> I am conducting independent historical land-use research on Ladera Ranch. I am writing to ask for
> your voluntary help. **I have no legal right to these records and I am not claiming one** — the
> Public Records Act does not apply to a private association, and I recognise this is entirely the
> association's decision.
>
> **The one thing I would ask even if you decline everything else:** please consider **preserving**
> the records below rather than letting them go to routine destruction while this research is under
> way. That costs nothing and keeps options open.
>
> [same numbered list as Version A]
>
> **What I am not asking for.** No homeowner names, addresses, accounts, or any individual member
> information of any kind.
>
> I am not alleging wrongdoing by the association or by any vendor, and nothing in this research
> assumes that any product caused any harm. I would be glad to speak with the Board or the managing
> agent about scope, and to accept any subset you are comfortable providing.
>
> Sincerely,
> [NAME] · [CONTACT] · [DATE]

## Follow-on: G05, the vendors themselves

Item 1 exists to yield **vendor names**. Once known, send each vendor the same preservation-first
voluntary request for their own service records — vendors often retain route and product logs the
association never held. That follow-on cannot be drafted until the names are known.

## Do not claim from any response
An application record shows that a product was applied at a place and time. It establishes
**nothing** about drift, persistence in soil, human contact, dose, or health outcome. Do not
characterise any vendor or the association as a source of harm.
