# G11 — Florida public-records request: complete cattle dip vat inventory

**To:** Florida Department of Environmental Protection — Public Records Coordinator
(public.services@floridadep.gov; cc: Waste Management District Business Support)
**Cc (parallel request):** Florida Department of Health — Public Records
**Statute:** Chapter 119, Florida Statutes (Florida Public Records Act)

---

Subject: Public records request — cattle dipping vat inventory and location records

Dear Records Coordinator,

Under Chapter 119, Florida Statutes, I request copies of the following public records:

1. The Department's complete inventory of known and suspected cattle dipping vat (CDV)
   locations statewide, in machine-readable form if maintained (database export, GIS
   layer/shapefile/geodatabase, or spreadsheet), including any county-by-county lists.
   Historical context: FDEP and FDOH materials reference approximately 3,200–3,400 vats
   constructed 1906–1962, and the FDOH formerly published a "Cattle dip vats — location
   by county" page (floridahealth.gov/environmental-health/drinking-water/countyvat.html)
   that is no longer accessible.
2. The site inventory, prioritization records, and appendices of the Cattle Dip Vat
   Assessment Program created pursuant to the 1994 legislation (HB 1959 / SB 2550),
   including the Woodward-Clyde final report appendices listing assessed and candidate
   sites.
3. Any GIS data layer(s) used by the Department to track CDV sites, including those
   underlying the "FDEP Listed Cattle Dip Vats" list (45 sites) published on the
   Department's website.
4. Records sufficient to identify any known or suspected CDV locations in **Palm Beach
   County**, including sites identified during the fever-tick program's final regulated
   period (through October 1961).

I am an independent environmental-history researcher; this request supports a public,
non-commercial research project on the national legacy of the federal tick-eradication
program. I request electronic delivery. If any portion is exempt, please release the
non-exempt remainder and cite the specific exemption. Please provide a fee estimate
before incurring charges above $25.

Thank you for your assistance.

Andy Stavros
andystavros@icloud.com
