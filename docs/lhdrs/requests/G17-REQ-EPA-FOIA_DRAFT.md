## What this seeks and what is already public

Gate G17 concerns correspondence from a U.S. Attorney to EPA regarding this matter, and EPA's
response. Public statements exist; the underlying correspondence does not appear to be public.

**Honesty requirement:** this project has **not** independently verified the existence, date, or
contents of that correspondence beyond public reporting. The request below is therefore written to
ask **whether responsive records exist**, not to assert that they do. Do not edit it into a
statement of fact.

## Do not claim from the response
The existence of correspondence establishes that someone wrote to someone. It establishes
**nothing** about contamination, causation, or the merits of any concern. A "no responsive records"
response likewise does not disprove anything.
