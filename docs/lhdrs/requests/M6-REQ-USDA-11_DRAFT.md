**Not a PRA request.** This is a customer coverage inquiry to a federal program office, so it is
written as a plain enquiry rather than a statutory demand.

## Why the scope narrowed

Originally scoped 2003–2008. Two things have since changed:
- The CDFW public mirror exposes study-period NAIP services for **2005, 2009 and 2010**, so those
  need not be requested. That mirror result does not prove USDA lacks other acquisitions.
- Mission 7 acquired **USGS High Resolution Orthoimagery for 2004-01-21 at 0.3 m, covering 100% of
  the AOI**. A NAIP frame from later in 2004 would still add value as a **second observation within
  the same year** during peak build-out, so 2004 is retained — but the need is now corroborative
  rather than foundational, and that should be said plainly.

Residual ask: **2004** (second-half preferred) and **2006–2008**.

## Do not claim from the response
Absence of NAIP coverage for a year is **not** evidence that nothing occurred that year, and NAIP
imagery shows ground surface condition only — never soil chemistry, transport, or exposure.
