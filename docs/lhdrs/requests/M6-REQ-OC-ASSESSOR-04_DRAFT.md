## Why this request exists

The County parcel layer carries a `YEAR_BUILT` field, but it is **blank for 6,041 of 6,101 parcels
inside the Ladera Ranch CDP** — populated for four, about 0.1%. That field is therefore unusable as
a construction or occupancy chronology. This request asks whether the Assessor's own records carry
the characteristic data that the public parcel layer does not expose.

## Do not claim from the response
Assessor year-built and improvement fields are **supporting indicators only**. They do not prove
permit final dates or certificate-of-occupancy dates and may never be substituted for them.
