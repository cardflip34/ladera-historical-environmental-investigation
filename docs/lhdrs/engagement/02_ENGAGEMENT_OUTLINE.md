# Engagement outline — research consulting & data license

*Term sheet for discussion between Andy Stavros ("Consultant") and Bond Legal PC ("Firm").
This is a discussion draft, not a contract, and not legal advice. Both parties should have
counsel review any final agreement. Prepared 2026-08-10.*

## 1. Role
Consultant is retained as an independent **research and site-targeting consultant**
(consulting, non-testifying capacity). Any testifying role would require a separate,
later agreement. Consultant is not an employee, agent, or investigator of the Firm.

## 2. Scope of services (ongoing work, billed hourly)
- Historical land-use research and source acquisition (graded, hash-verified custody).
- Geospatial analysis: imagery interpretation 1929–present, site targeting, mapping.
- Soils-sampling support: target selection rationale, review of sampling plans, liaison
  with the environmental consultant on site-selection questions.
- Public-records strategy: drafted requests, gap tracking, follow-up analysis.
- Briefings and written summaries for the Firm's team.

## 3. Pre-existing intellectual property ("the Corpus")
All research created by Consultant before the effective date — including the source
registry and archive, quantitative models and their outputs, imagery analyses, maps,
site-targeting analyses, field observations, reports, and videos — is and remains
**Consultant's sole property**, independently created and timestamped.

**License:** Consultant grants the Firm a **non-exclusive, non-transferable license**
(extendable to formally associated co-counsel at no charge) to use the Corpus in
connection with this matter only, **with attribution** to Consultant in any use.
No public release of Corpus materials by the Firm without Consultant's written consent.
The license does not restrict Consultant's own continued research or publication,
subject only to §7 confidentiality regarding Firm work product.

## 4. New work product
Work created at the Firm's direction under this engagement is licensed to the Firm for
the matter. Underlying methods, tools, and data compilations remain Consultant's.

## 5. Compensation (all non-contingent)
- **Corpus license fee:** flat fee on signing (see rate card), payable regardless of
  matter outcome. Covers delivery of the targeting package and existing reports.
- **Consulting services:** hourly at the standard rate (see rate card), invoiced monthly,
  net 30. Site days at the day rate. Expenses reimbursed at cost with receipts.
- **No contingency:** no part of Consultant's compensation is contingent on the outcome
  of any claim. (This protects the admissibility and credibility of the research.)

## 6. Independence and methodology
Consultant's work remains **hypothesis-neutral**: findings are reported with source
grades and confidence labels, whatever they show. Consultant will not alter methods,
grades, or conclusions to fit litigation strategy. The Firm may choose what to use;
it may not direct what the research concludes.

## 7. Confidentiality — with carve-outs
Consultant will maintain confidentiality of Firm work product and litigation strategy.
Carve-outs, non-negotiable:
- **(a) Public-health disclosure:** nothing in this engagement delays or restricts
  disclosure of findings indicating potential health hazards (e.g., hazardous soil
  concentrations) to appropriate public authorities.
- **(b) Pre-existing public materials:** anything Consultant published or timestamped
  before the effective date remains outside confidentiality.
- **(c) Post-matter publication:** Consultant may publish the research after the matter
  concludes, or earlier with consent not unreasonably withheld.

## 8. The independent testing track
The parties acknowledge the community/council soils-testing effort is independent of
this engagement. Consultant may continue to support it. The Firm will not seek to
control, delay, or condition that effort through this agreement.

## 9. Term and termination
Either party may terminate on 30 days' notice. Earned fees and the license fee are
non-refundable; the matter license survives for materials already delivered. Attribution
and §7 carve-outs survive termination.

## 10. Miscellaneous
Governing law: California. Disputes: meet-and-confer, then mediation before litigation.
This outline supersedes nothing and binds no one until a definitive agreement is signed.
