# Andy Stavros — Independent Environmental-History Researcher
## Ladera Ranch Historical Dipping-Era Research Platform (LEHRP/LHDRS)

*One-page project curriculum vitae. Shareable. Prepared 2026-08-10.*

**Role:** Founder and sole researcher of a two-year independent investigation into the
1907–1912 compulsory arsenical cattle-dipping era on the historic O'Neill Ranch (present-day
Ladera Ranch / Arroyo Trabuco corridor, South Orange County, CA) and its potential relevance
to reported pediatric cancers. All work is source-graded, provenance-tracked, timestamped in
a version-controlled repository, and deliberately hypothesis-neutral.

### Research corpus (as of August 2026)
- **Historical program documentation** — USDA Bureau of Animal Industry circulars (1911–1912)
  held in full text with SHA-256 custody manifests: dip formulas (8 lb arsenic trioxide per
  500 gal), federal vat construction specifications, county-level enforcement records
  including Orange County passages.
- **Quantitative exposure modeling** — arsenic mass-balance from documented herd sizes
  (~21,000–25,000 head), vat-count requirements, drag-out and grazing-distribution models;
  all outputs labeled as model estimates with stated assumptions.
- **Imagery archive & analysis** — 21 registered aerial/satellite epochs (1929–2025) spanning
  nine sources, incl. county 7.6 cm/px flights, USGS 30 cm orthoimagery, USDA NAIP with
  infrared analysis; systematic structure searches, change detection, water-source mapping.
- **Federal survey integration** — 1968 USGS field-mapped stock-water layer; drainage and
  depositional-zone modeling for sampling-site selection.
- **Field reconnaissance** — documented site visits (2026) with photographic evidence chains:
  historic fencing, structural remnants, candidate features; drone survey.
- **Soils sampling plan** — prioritized, coordinate-level target package incl. a dated-sediment
  site designed to separate ranch-era from modern arsenic sources.
- **Public-records strategy** — 10 drafted agency requests (county, state, federal archives)
  mapped to specific evidence gaps.
- **Health-pattern framing** — timing-consistency modeling (Ewing sarcoma age-distribution
  convolution), maintained at aggregate level only, with explicit statements of what the
  data cannot yet establish.

### Methods & tools
GIS/geospatial analysis (GDAL/rasterio ecosystem), historical imagery registration,
archival research, statistical modeling, chain-of-custody documentation, structured
source grading (A1–D), public-records law (CPRA/FOIA drafting).
