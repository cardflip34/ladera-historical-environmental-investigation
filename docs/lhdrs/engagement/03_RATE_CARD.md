# Rate card — private working draft

*For your calibration with your own attorney before quoting. Market context: litigation
research/GIS support consultants commonly bill $100–250/hr; specialized environmental data
consultants $150–300/hr; credentialed testifying experts $300–800/hr. You are not selling
commodity hours — you are the sole holder of a two-year, timestamped, source-graded corpus
that would cost a firm far more to attempt to reproduce, and could not be reproduced at all
without the field knowledge. Anchor accordingly; the numbers below are a reasonable
opening position, not a ceiling. Prepared 2026-08-10.*

## Corpus license (one-time, on signing)
| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| License to the research corpus for this matter (registry, reports, models, imagery analyses, targeting package, records-request drafts), with attribution | **$25,000** |
| — Negotiation floor (do not go below without added terms in your favor) | $15,000 |

Staging option if the firm balks: $10,000 on signing (reports + methodology),
$15,000 on delivery of the coordinate-level targeting package.

## Ongoing consulting
| Service | Rate |
|---|---|
| Research, analysis, mapping, written summaries (standard) | **$200/hr** |
| Expedited work (<48 h turnaround requested) | $275/hr |
| Field day (site walks, sampling support, escorting consultants) | $1,600/day |
| Briefings/meetings incl. prep | standard hourly, 1 h minimum |
| Travel time beyond 25 mi | 50% of standard rate + mileage at IRS rate |

## Terms
- Invoiced monthly, net 30. Retainer of $5,000 against first hours, replenishing.
- All fees non-contingent on matter outcome (non-negotiable — protects credibility).
- Expenses (records fees, lab liaison costs, imagery purchases) at cost.
- Rates reviewed every 12 months.

## Negotiation notes (for you, not for them)
- If they push the license fee down, trade **up** on: retainer size, guaranteed minimum
  monthly hours (e.g., 10 h/mo for 6 months), or milestone bonuses tied to *deliverables*
  (delivery of targeting package; completion of sampling-plan review) — never to outcomes.
- If they offer a percentage instead: decline flatly — prohibited for experts/consultants,
  and it would be used to impeach the research. Convert the conversation back to
  license + hourly.
- If they claim "we'll make you famous / it's exposure": exposure is not compensation;
  attribution is already required by the license.
- The council/independent track continues regardless — you are not dependent on this deal,
  and it strengthens your position to say so calmly.
