## EM-012 — Arsenical cattle dipping actually occurred within the Ladera Ranch footprint

**Confidence:** None  ·  **Status:** OPEN

**Supporting evidence.** No direct evidence. Terrain logic places working facilities near water and trail convergences, and one such node exists.

**Limiting or contradictory evidence.** No vat found on adequate imagery. No documentary record located. No soil sampling has tested for it in the residential footprint. This is an UNPROVEN HYPOTHESIS and must not be presented as more.

**Citation.** No supporting citation exists - this is the central open question of the investigation


## EM-018 — No 1912 plat map of Rancho Santa Margarita y Las Flores has been located; the available survey plat is dated 1867

**Confidence:** Medium  ·  **Status:** Open

**Supporting evidence.** Searched Library of Congress maps and JSON API, David Rumsey, Calisphere, Online Archive of California, and archive.org. Only the 1867 Strobel survey plat surfaced (Bancroft Library, Land Case Map E-1362A, 89,742 acres). Acquired.

**Limiting or contradictory evidence.** BLM GLO Records could NOT be searched - the current site requires Login.gov authentication and no account was created. GLO remains the most likely repository for an early-20th-century plat. Absence here reflects an unsearched archive, NOT a demonstrated absence. Structurally, 1912 postdates the 1879 patent, so any such document would more likely be a partition or subdivision map than a Surveyor General plat.

**Citation.** Bancroft Library Land Case Map E-1362A, ark:/13030/hb0199n5n8; negative searches logged in DEAD_ENDS.md


## EM-021 — No Orange County newspaper covering the 1908-1912 quarantine period has been digitised in any searched database

**Confidence:** High  ·  **Status:** Open

**Supporting evidence.** CDNC holds only four Orange County titles and none covers 1900-1909. Chronicling America holds ZERO Orange County California titles, verified against a control (15 titles for Los Angeles County, 1 for San Diego). Every Orange County finding in this investigation came from OUT-OF-COUNTY papers reporting inward - the Los Angeles Herald and Pacific Rural Press.

**Limiting or contradictory evidence.** This is a DIGITISATION GAP, NOT AN ABSENCE OF RECORDS. The Santa Ana Blade and Anaheim Gazette are confirmed by name in a 1903 Pacific Rural Press item as the papers of record; neither is digitised anywhere searched. Quarantine proclamations would have been published as paid legal notices in exactly those titles. Their absence online says nothing about whether they exist on microfilm.

**Citation.** Coverage audit of CDNC and Chronicling America holdings, this investigation; see DEAD_ENDS.md DE-011


## EM-025 — The hazardous-materials assessment for the land Ladera Ranch was built on has been identified by name but is not available online

**Confidence:** High  ·  **Status:** Open - TOP PRIORITY RECORDS REQUEST

**Supporting evidence.** EIR 589 Appendix I, p.21, cites: 'Michael Brandman Associates, May 1995, Environmental Impact Report for the Antonio Parkway Roadway Alignment and Land Use Plan, Appendix H - Hazardous Materials Environmental Site Assessment.' A complete half-year CEQAnet sweep (22 queries, lead agency Orange County, 1990-2000; plus 14 by county 1993-1999) returned zero Ladera Ranch records. CEQAnet hosts no attachments for filings of that vintage.

**Limiting or contradictory evidence.** The identification of SCH 1994031075 (Antonio Parkway Alignment Study) as the Ladera Ranch entitlement vehicle is INFERRED from geography and a single citation, and is UNCONFIRMED. The document may or may not address cattle-era land use; its contents are unknown.

**Citation.** EIR 589 Appendix I, p.21; CEQAnet SCH 1994031075. Records request: Orange County Planning, (714) 834-2166


## EM-028 — The terms and dates of the Forster-to-O'Neill/Flood transfers remain unverified

**Confidence:** High  ·  **Status:** Open

**Supporting evidence.** RESOLVED SINCE FIRST ENTRY: the grant date (4 Apr 1845), grantee (Agustin Olvera) and patent date (6 Aug 1866) are now established from Hoffman (1862) and the California Secretary of State. What remains open is the later transfer chain.

**Limiting or contradictory evidence.** Secondary accounts conflict on every material detail and none has been checked against a deed. US Land Commission case files - which frequently contain survey plats and testimony describing IMPROVEMENTS AND STRUCTURES, precisely what this investigation needs - are held by the National Archives and Bancroft and have NOT been obtained. BLM GLO records remain blocked behind Login.gov (DE-007).

**Citation.** Gap identified from Armor (1921); primary sources not obtained
