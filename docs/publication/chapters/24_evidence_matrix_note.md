# Evidence Matrix

The complete matrix follows this chapter as a **searchable, filterable section** — every claim in
this investigation with its classification, confidence, supporting evidence, limiting or
contradictory evidence, citation, and status.

It is generated directly from `research/_logs/EVIDENCE_MATRIX.csv`, so the published matrix and
the working data cannot drift apart.

---

## How to read it

**Four classifications**, and the distinction between them does most of the work in this report:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Established Fact** | Documented in a primary or official source, verified against it, reproducible by following the citation |
| **Historical Context** | Accurate background that situates the investigation but is **not a finding about this land** |
| **Investigative Lead** | A testable proposition with partial support and a defined next step. Not a finding |
| **Open Question** | Material, identified, and genuinely unresolved |

**Confidence** is separate from classification. A claim can be an established fact at *low*
confidence — EM-003, that no vat was found in the imagery, is exactly that: the observation is
solid, its evidential weight is not.

**The counter-evidence field is mandatory.** Where a claim has no meaningful counter-evidence,
that is stated explicitly rather than left blank, because an empty field is ambiguous between
"none exists" and "none was sought."
