# Limitations

A limitations section that lists only minor caveats is a marketing document. This one is
intended to be usable by someone trying to determine how much weight this publication can bear.

Several of the limitations below are severe enough that they should change how a reader reads
everything preceding them.

---

## 31.1 Structural limitations — these do not improve with more work

**This is not an epidemiological study.** No individual health data were collected, requested,
or analysed. Whether the reported cases constitute a statistical excess is not determined here,
and no analysis in this publication is capable of determining it. Any reader looking for that
answer will not find it here, and should not infer it from anything that is here.

**Spatial coincidence is not exposure.** Every geographic finding establishes proximity and
nothing else. It does not establish that a substance was present, that it persisted, that anyone
contacted it, or in what quantity. The chain from *proximity* to *exposure* to *dose* to *harm*
has three unbridged links, and this publication crosses none of them.

**Ecological inference cannot transfer to individuals.** Community-scale patterns say nothing
about any individual. This is a permanent property of the data, not a temporary gap.

**Absence of online records is not absence of records.** This investigation is conducted almost
entirely from publicly reachable sources. Documents held in county file cabinets, private
corporate archives, or un-digitised collections are invisible to it. **Every statement in this
publication about what "does not appear" in the record is a statement about what this
investigation could reach, not about what exists.**

## 31.2 Evidentiary limitations

**The imagery post-dates the period of interest.** The earliest photography is 1929; compulsory
dipping ran to about 1917. Twelve years is a substantial improvement on the thirty-one this
project previously believed, but it does not close the gap. A facility decommissioned and
backfilled in 1917 could be entirely invisible by 1929. The negative imagery finding is therefore
**bounded**: it is meaningful evidence against a *surviving surface facility*, and no evidence at
all about a *demolished* one.

**The central premise is unverified.** Whether California's tick eradication programme applied to
Orange County at all has not been confirmed against primary sources. Two claims in the evidence
matrix (EM-011, EM-014) are explicitly flagged **PENDING VERIFICATION** for this reason. A
reader should treat the entire dipping hypothesis as resting on an unconfirmed foundation until
that flag is cleared.

**No soil measurement exists for the residential footprint.** Every statement about arsenic in
this study area derives from sampling at *school sites*, which are different parcels with
different histories. Nothing in this publication reports a measurement from Ladera Ranch soil,
because none was located.

**Named ranch facilities remain unlocated.** Headquarters, cow camps, line camps, and named
corrals are referenced in the historical record but none has been located with coordinates by
this investigation. Whether any stood within the study footprint — as opposed to elsewhere on a
ranch covering well over a hundred thousand acres — is unresolved.

## 31.3 Data quality limitations

**Pesticide use reporting cannot resolve to this community.** 94.6% of 79,473 Orange County
records could not be located more precisely than a large grid cell. Within landscape maintenance
specifically, **22 of 15,383 records (0.1%)** carried usable coordinates. The landscape-pesticide
pathway is not weakly supported — it is **unmeasurable** with the available instrument, which is
a different and more honest statement.

**Building footprints are community-mapped.** OpenStreetMap completeness is not guaranteed
uniform. For the water-proximity analysis this biases measured distances *upward*, making that
result conservative — but the same caveat would not necessarily be favourable for a different
analysis.

**The water layer is derived, not digitised from survey data.** It was extracted by colour
threshold from a scanned printed sheet. It inherits cartographic generalisation and scan
artefacts. Positions are centroids, not outlines. Features under 350 m² were excluded by design
and are absent regardless of whether they existed.

**Study zones are approximate.** Zone A is a bounding box, not a cadastral boundary. Any analysis
sensitive to its exact edges should not use it.

## 31.4 Methodological failures recorded

Recorded because a limitations section that omits the methods that failed is not complete.

**Automated water detection failed and was abandoned.** A detector keyed on brightness and
texture returned roughly 100 candidates inside Zone A and was found to be detecting hillslope
shadow. None of its output appears in this publication.

**Automated structure detection was 4% accurate.** A blob detector on the 1948 sheet returned 25
candidates; 24 were map-label letters, section-line dots, and a benchmark mark. Only visual
verification prevented them from being published as findings.

**A terrain analysis was invalidated by its own subject.** Buildings sit at median 1.7° slope
against 6.7° for available land — a striking figure that cannot be used, because mass grading
manufactured the flatness being measured.

**A derived drainage network was too sparse to use.** The flow-accumulation threshold was set too
conservatively, producing a channel network inconsistent with the visible drainage pattern. The
valley-bottom enrichment figure computed from it is not reported anywhere as a finding.

## 31.5 Limitations of the author and method

**No formal training.** This investigation was conducted by a community member without training
in epidemiology, toxicology, geology, or archival research, using AI tools to organise and
analyse public records. Domain experts will find things here that a specialist would have caught
sooner, framed better, or avoided entirely.

**AI-assisted analysis carries characteristic failure modes.** These include fluent presentation
of uncertain conclusions, plausible-sounding detail that is not grounded in a source, and
confident restatement of an earlier error. The project's countermeasures — mandatory
counter-evidence on every claim, a published dead-end log, source grading with no silent
promotion, and open corrections — are mitigations, not guarantees. **Readers should verify claims
that matter to them against the cited sources rather than relying on this document.**

**Confirmation bias is the standing risk.** This investigation began with a hypothesis. Two
significant results have already cut against it: the corrected imagery premise, and the failed
water-proximity hypothesis. Both were retained. That is the intended behaviour, and it is not
evidence that the bias has been eliminated — only that it was caught twice.

## 31.6 What would change the conclusions

Stated so the publication is falsifiable rather than merely cautious:

| Finding | Effect |
|---|---|
| Documentation that Orange County was outside the tick quarantine area | **Closes the hypothesis entirely** |
| A period document, photograph, or account placing a vat on this ranch | Converts the central lead into a finding |
| Soil arsenic sharply elevated in a small area **without** proportionate lead | Strong support for a point source |
| Soil arsenic elevated broadly **with** proportionate lead | Points to orchard lead arsenate, not dipping |
| Geophysics finding buried concrete at the Trabuco node | Materially raises priority for sampling |
| A Phase I ESA showing the historical review reached the 1920s and found nothing | Substantially reduces the open question |
| Local background arsenic at or above measured concentrations | Removes the anthropogenic inference |

## 31.7 The honest summary

This publication reconstructs what the reachable public record shows about the history of this
land, and identifies where that record has gaps.

It does not establish that anything harmful is present in Ladera Ranch soil. It does not
establish that anyone was exposed to anything. It does not explain the reported cases, and it
does not attempt to.

Its most defensible product is a **map of a specific uncertainty** — a bounded set of locations
and a set of documentary questions, each with a method attached and a defined outcome in both
directions.

That is less than the investigation set out to find. It is what the evidence supports.
