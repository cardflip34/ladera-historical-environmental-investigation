# Irvine / Irvine Ranch — recon findings (2026-07-21)

> Hypothesis-neutral. No contamination claimed. Working file — not part of the publication.

## Headline
Irvine has the **strongest cattle-*operations* profile of the three communities** — a named
cattle-handling headquarters — **but no located record names a dip vat here, and the arsenic that
does appear in Irvine soils tracks with orchards, not grazing.** The question is open, not answered.

## The cattle-handling node (why Irvine is a strong candidate)
- **Bommer Canyon Cattle Camp** was the Irvine Ranch's *southern cattle headquarters* (~1870s–1970):
  spring roundups, "processing," corrals, **loading ramps**, holding pens. Surviving relics:
  "several buildings, loading ramps, corrals, and equipment." *[Irvine Ranch Conservancy, B1]*
- Corrals + loading ramps + processing + spring water = exactly the co-location where a period dip
  vat would sit. This makes Irvine a stronger operations candidate than the Newport Coast grazing
  slopes.

## What was NOT found
- **No located record of a dip vat, dipping, or arsenical/lime-sulfur treatment** at Bommer Canyon
  or any Irvine Ranch facility — not in the Conservancy pages, City park pages, Irvine Standard
  features, or Wikipedia. Absence of a located record ≠ absence of fact. **Cleland (1952), *The
  Irvine Ranch of Orange County* — the definitive ranch history — has NOT been page-checked and is
  the single most important next document.**
- **EnviroStor (66 Irvine sites) + GeoTracker (379): none is a dip vat.** A *statewide* EnviroStor
  search for "DIP VAT"/"DIPPING" = **0**; the only animal-dip comparator anywhere is the "Presidio of
  San Francisco / Former Horse Dip Area" — proving DTSC *does* register arsenical dip sites when
  documented, under the site name. *[A1, live REST query]*

## The strongest counter-evidence yet (arsenic tracks orchards, not grazing)
- DTSC school-site investigations on former Irvine Ranch **orchard/row-crop** land repeatedly find
  **arsenic co-occurring with organochlorine pesticides (DDT/chlordane) + lead** — the classic
  **lead-arsenate orchard residue** suite (Woodbridge HS, Woodbury, Orchard Hills, others). *[A1]*
- **Pure former-*grazing*-only parcels test clean:** Laguna Niguel Elem., San Juan Hills HS, Turtle
  Ridge (methane only) — and, tying back to prior work, **Oso Grande School in Ladera Ranch:
  livestock past-use, "No Contaminants found."** *[A1]* → In south OC, arsenic tracks with
  orchards/row crops, not with grazing. This is the strongest single counter to the dip hypothesis.
- **BUT two caveats keep it open:** (a) a dip vat's fingerprint is *high, localized arsenic WITHOUT
  the OCP suite* — distinguishable from orchard residue, but the aggregate databases don't resolve it;
  (b) **Bommer Canyon — the prime candidate — was never developed, so it never triggered a CEQA
  Phase I or DTSC drilling.** The very place to look has had the *least* investigation.

## Imagery & review gap
- Earliest topo **1896/1901** (pre-window), next **1932**; aerials **1927/1929/1938**. A vat is below
  topo resolution regardless. **But the cattle camp operated to ~1970, so a durable corral/vat complex
  would appear on 1927–1970 aerials** → a targeted aerial screen of Bommer Canyon is the recommended
  low-cost step. *(Pipeline now built — `scripts/pull_oc_historical_aerial.py`; 1931 first look done,
  see `IMAGERY_NOTES.md`.)*
- **The real review gap is at the prime candidate site:** undeveloped Bommer Canyon = effectively zero
  subsurface investigation.
