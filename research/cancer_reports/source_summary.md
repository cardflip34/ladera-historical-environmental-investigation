## Core established facts (Officially reported / Confirmed)

- **An official multi-agency data review is underway.** OC Health Care Agency's County
  Health Officer initiated outreach; representatives of the **California Cancer Registry**,
  **UC Irvine Cancer Center**, and the **OC Agricultural Commissioner's Office** agreed to
  conduct an **updated review of cancer data**, with findings pending. *(Grade A2 statement,
  reported across multiple B2 outlets.)*
- **The initial data review did not identify a particular pattern** of cancer cases; a
  further review is planned. *(OC HCA.)*
- **A federal investigation was requested, not confirmed opened.** First Assistant U.S.
  Attorney **Bill Essayli** asked **EPA** to investigate on **2026-07-17**. No EPA response
  found as of retrieval.
- **LARMAC** (Ladera Ranch Maintenance Corporation) announced a **60-day pause** on certain
  routine landscape weed-management/plant-growth products in common areas and formed an ad
  hoc advisory committee. LARMAC stated it is not aware of any agency determination linking
  its landscape practices to the illnesses.
- **Two distinct cancer types** are publicly reported among affected youths: **Ewing
  sarcoma** (multiple) and **one synovial sarcoma**. **One death** is publicly reported
  (Ewing sarcoma → secondary AML, age ~17, March 2026).
- Elected officials publicly engaged: Asm. Kate Sanchez, Asm. Diane Dixon, Supervisor
  Katrina Foley. A **separate, earlier** county pesticide-politics track (Foley's 5/14
  herbicide pause; the 6/23 public-noticing vote; Dixon's 5/15 letter) **predates and does
  not reference** the cancer cluster.

## Case-count discrepancy (a data-quality finding in itself)

| Count claimed | Framing | Source | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| "At least 6" Ewing sarcoma since 2013 | Ladera Ranch only, Ewing only | NBC LA (SRC-NEWS-001) | B2 |
| "About a dozen" rare cancers | **Mixed cancer types AND multiple OC cities** | NBC LA (SRC-NEWS-002/003) | B2 |
| "12 cases of Ewing sarcoma" | Highest Ewing-specific count | California Post (SRC-NEWS-007) | C |

**Key point:** the widely-repeated "dozen" is **not** a dozen Ewing sarcoma cases — NBC's
"about a dozen" explicitly mixes cancer types and multiple cities. Only "at least 6" refers
to Ewing sarcoma in Ladera Ranch. The "12 Ewing" figure is a single low-reliability outlier.

## Alleged / unverified (claims, not agency findings)

- **Causation by pesticides/herbicides** — NO agency has found or asserted any causal link;
  explicitly disclaimed by LARMAC and framed as unproven by NBC and CDPH.
- **"17 different pesticides applied in June, almost daily"** — attributed to attorney
  Jackie French (Bond Legal, a resident) characterizing records she reviewed, via NY Post;
  not an official records release. LARMAC reportedly declined to release the records.
- **Glufosinate / "Lifeline" and "Attrimec"** as culprits — named by advocacy (Toxic-Free
  OC), a LARMAC scheduling notice, and Epoch Times; not identified as causal by any agency.
  No established scientific link between glufosinate and Ewing sarcoma.
- **That a true "cancer cluster" exists** — not confirmed; agencies are at the review stage.
