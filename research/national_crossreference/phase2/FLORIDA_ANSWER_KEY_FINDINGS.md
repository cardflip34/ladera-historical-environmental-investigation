# Florida answer-key findings — first pass (2026-08-11)

## What Florida's own records show (all A2 — state documents held locally)

1. **When Florida tested its vat sites, 11 of 12 exceeded acceptable soil concentrations**
   (92%) and required consideration for soil remediation. One produced an extensive
   groundwater plume (Walker Ranch). Assessment costs ran $24k–$190k per site;
   remediation $40k–$450k. (Woodward-Clyde / FDEP Cattle Dip Vat Assessment Program
   final report, held: FDEP_CDV_Assessment_Report.pdf, 58 pp.)
2. **The formula matches our federal circulars exactly**: ~24 lb sodium carbonate +
   8 lb arsenic trioxide + 1 gal pine tar per mix — independent cross-validation of the
   LEHRP mass-balance inputs. Drip pads 12×15 ft, vats 3.5–6.5 ft deep: same specs.
3. **Documented secondary uses** of disused vats include "mixing areas for pesticides and
   swimming holes for children" — the state's own report.
4. **The DEP cleanup list (45 tracked vats, held as CSV) already contains the failure mode**:
   - CATTLE DIPPING VAT / STONEYBROOK DEVELOPMENT (Orange Co.)
   - PEACH CREEK DEVELOPMENT cattle dip vat (Walton Co., Santa Rosa Beach)
   - ELEMENTARY SCHOOL Q cattle dip vat (Clay Co., Green Cove Springs)
   Florida's registry proves vats end up inside developments and at schools — the U.S.
   equivalent of NSW's built-over homes.
5. **Palm Beach County ran dipping the longest**: among the last "regulated" counties;
   last fever-tick outbreak in the state was 1960 on Palm Beach cattle; the state program
   ran to October 1961. The Acreage (FDOH-declared pediatric cluster, 1994–2010,
   unresolved) sits in the county with the state's LONGEST dipping history.

## The open question (not yet answered — do not overstate)
The DEP cleanup list shows only one tracked vat in Palm Beach County (Reese, Jupiter,
~20 km from The Acreage). The cleanup list is 45 sites; the historical inventory is ~3,400.
Whether any of the ~3,400 known-vat locations fall in or near The Acreage requires the full
FDOH/DEP inventory (the FDOH county page has moved; next step: archived copies, DEP GIS
layers, or a records request to FDEP — draft ready to generate). Finding no vat near The
Acreage would be reported just as prominently as finding one.
