## Modern location — GEOREFERENCED (BLM PLSS CadNSDI polygons, this session) ⚠ CORRECTS PRIOR CLAIM
The **actual survey polygons** were pulled from BLM CadNSDI (township PLSSID `CA270060S0070W0`, SBM
principal-meridian code 27) and overlaid on Esri imagery (`joplin_patent_section_overlay.jpg`):
- **Section 24** centroid **33.63616, −117.56206**; extent lon −117.5735…−117.5564, lat 33.6283…33.6428.
- **Section 25** (a fractional sliver on the south edge) centroid **33.62801, −117.55683**.

**What the overlay shows:** Joplin's patented homestead core lies over the **northeastern part of the
built Coto de Caza community** — its western half covers **residential streets, golf-course fairways, and
a reservoir**; its eastern half is **open chaparral foothill**. **It is NOT the Starr Ranch Sanctuary**,
which sits ~2–3 mi further **east**.

### ⚠ This corrects the earlier "preserved, not built over" conclusion
The prior note (`JOPLIN_DIP_SITE_LOCATED.md`) concluded the documented dip site = Starr Ranch (protected
open space), reasoning from the Starr history's line that Starr bought 300 ac "from the Joplin family in
1927." The patent shows that conflated two different parcels:
- **Joplin's PATENTED HOMESTEAD** (where he *resided* — and where a ranch's dip vat, sited at the home
  working corral, is most likely) = **Sec 24/25 = today's NE Coto de Caza** (partly built, partly foothill).
- The **Joplin family's broader ~766-ac holdings** (Armor 1921) extended **east** into Bell Canyon; the
  **eastern** part is what Starr bought in 1927 → today's Starr Ranch.
So the honest, corrected reading: **the homestead core of the one documented Orange County dip ranch is
now partly under the Coto de Caza community (homes/golf/reservoir), not wholly preserved.** This makes the
Coto/Joplin case resemble the Ladera concern rather than being its reassuring opposite. *(Still: the exact
vat parcel within the homestead is unknown; no soil tested; a dip could also have sat on the eastern
foothill portion, which remains open. No contamination is asserted.)*

## Why this matters
- Upgrades the Joplin site from "the canyon" to an **exact survey section** — the unit a soil-sampling
  plan, a records request, or a parcel-history search is actually written against.
- Independently **corroborates Armor 1921 + the 1908 press**: a real federal homestead patent to the
  named man, in the right county, dated 1909 (Armor said "possessory claim … seventeen years," patented
  once he had title).
- The 1912 state quarantine district was **T6S R6W** (recovered proclamation); Joplin's patent is the
  adjoining **T6S R7W** — the two are neighbors, tightening the documented-dipping geography.
