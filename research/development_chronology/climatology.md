# Wind, Marine Influence, and Topographic Context

## Scope

This appendix provides regional physical context for the historical atlas. It does not
model contaminant transport and must not be used to infer exposure, dose, health risk, or
causation.

## Regional Wind Regimes

The National Weather Service's El Toro station summary reports a prevailing westerly wind
near 5 knots and describes dry easterly Santa Ana winds as a predominantly winter
phenomenon. El Toro is north of Ladera Ranch, so those values are regional context rather
than measurements inside individual Ladera valleys.

The National Weather Service describes Santa Ana winds as dry air flowing from inland high
pressure toward lower pressure off the coast, moving east to west through Southern
California mountain passages. The NWS identifies September through May as the most common
season. These episodic offshore winds differ from the ordinary onshore or westerly regime.

Sources: `LH-SRC-NWS-ELTORO` and `LH-SRC-NWS-SANTA-ANA`.

## Marine Layer

South Orange County is close enough to the Pacific for coastal low cloud and marine air to
affect ordinary conditions, but local timing and depth vary by season and terrain. No
site-specific marine-layer frequency has yet been assigned to Ladera Ranch. A defensible
quantitative appendix would require a validated nearby station and a stated period of
record.

## Terrain and Drainage

The community occupies rolling terrain and valley corridors associated with the San Juan
Creek watershed. Ridgelines and valleys can channel near-surface winds, so a regional
station direction cannot be applied uniformly to every village. The current 3DEP elevation
model also reflects extensive post-development grading inside the community; it cannot be
treated as the original 1997 landform.

Historical topographic maps and predevelopment imagery remain the preferred evidence for
original terrain interpretation. Reconstructing pre-grading contours is an open GIS task.

## Limitations

- No pollutant, plume, deposition, or transport model is included.
- No wind direction is assigned to a specific residence, school, or construction site.
- El Toro and John Wayne Airport are candidate regional stations, not validated local
  proxies for every Ladera Ranch valley.
- Modern elevation within the developed footprint is not a predevelopment surface.
