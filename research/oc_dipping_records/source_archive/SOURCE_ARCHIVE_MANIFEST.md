# Source archive — arsenical cattle dipping, primary documents

Every document this investigation's dipping-era findings rest on, held locally with full extracted
text so quotations can be verified offline and without depending on any website staying up.

| File | What it is | Grade | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `usda_bai_circular174_1911_eradicating_cattle_ticks_in_CALIFORNIA.pdf` | USDA Bureau of Animal Industry Circular 174 (1911), *Eradicating Cattle Ticks in California* | **A1** | **The key document.** Names Orange County in the heavily-infested tier; records ~21,000 head on one large ranch still quarantined in 1910; gives the 8 lb/500 gal formula and the 9–10 lb range-cattle escalation; documents the 2,088 gal swim vat; states wooden vats commonly leak |
| `usda_bai_circular183_1911_dipping_vat.pdf` | BAI Circular 183 (1911), dipping vat construction | A1 | vat dimensions and materials |
| `usda_bai_circular207_1912_dipping_vats.pdf` | BAI Circular 207 (1912), dipping vats | A1 | vat capacities; published as the CA programme closed |
| `usda_farmers_bulletin_1057_1919_cattle_fever_ticks_eradication.pdf` | Farmers' Bulletin 1057 (1919) | A1 | later programme practice |
| `usda_bai_1916_illustrated_lecture_cattle_tick_eradication.pdf` | BAI illustrated lecture (1916) | A1 | operational imagery |
| `proclamation_1912_johnson.pdf` | Governor Hiram Johnson proclamation, 1912 | A2 | the legal instrument ending the OC quarantine |

`extracted_text/` holds the full text of each, plus `_index.json` with page counts, character
counts and a **sha256 of every PDF** so any later copy can be verified against these.
