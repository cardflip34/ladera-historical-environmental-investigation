## The scorecard
- **1 of 15** states built a contamination registry (Florida).
- **1 of 15** built a partial cultural inventory (Arkansas).
- **13 of 15** — including California and Texas, the two biggest urbanization stories in
  the program footprint — recorded nothing.

## The California irony (inference, labeled)
California was released from quarantine EARLIEST (March 1912). Early release meant the
program was short, small in the state's memory, and completely undocumented at the site
level — while the land it touched (southern California ranchos) went on to host the
densest suburban build-out in the program's entire footprint. The states with the longest
programs (FL, TX, LA) kept dipping into living memory; California's five-year episode
fell out of institutional memory entirely. Shortest program, biggest build-out, zero
records: that combination is why nobody ever thought to look — and why LEHRP exists.
