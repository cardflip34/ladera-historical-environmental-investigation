# California Cattle Ranchos / Land Grants in the 1911 Cattle-Tick Quarantine Counties — Land-Use Context Reference

**Prepared:** 2026-07-21. **Purpose:** map-building reference for historical land-use context.
**Scope note:** This is a **land-use / historical-context compilation only**. It documents which
Mexican-/Spanish-era ranchos lay in the counties named in USDA BAI Circular 174 (1911) as under
cattle-tick quarantine (arsenical dipping, ~1907–1915), and what modern communities sit on that
land. **It makes no claim that any listed community has soil contamination, arsenical residue, or
any exposure** — that question is neither asserted nor established here.

**Source grading (per project SOURCE_POLICY):** Grant acreages below are cited to Wikipedia rancho
articles used **for orientation (grade C)**; however, the underlying acreage figures in those
articles are almost all taken from the **U.S. land-patent surveys / Ogden Hoffman *Reports of Land
Cases* (primary records, grade A1–A2)**. Where a company/foundation/historical-society source was
used it is noted. **Where sources conflict on acreage, both figures are reported.**

## Summary — where the O'Neill ranch ranked

At its ~200,000–210,000+ acre peak the **O'Neill–Flood (Santa Margarita Ranch)** operation ranked roughly
**third** among these named California giants: well behind the **Miller & Lux** farming empire (~1.4 million
acres in California) and the **Tejon Ranch** (270,000 contiguous acres), but ahead of the **Newhall Ranch**
(143,000 acres) and the **Irvine Ranch** (~110,000–125,000 acres). Within **coastal Southern California** it was
the largest single holding and was routinely called "the greatest of all California ranchos." Note that most of
its bulk was the **Santa Margarita y Las Flores** parcel, which after the 1939 split became **federal land
(Camp Pendleton)**; the Orange County remainder (Mission Viejo + Trabuco) is the ~23,000-acre present-day
Rancho Mission Viejo on which Ladera Ranch, Rancho Santa Margarita, Las Flores, and Mission Viejo were built.
