# Evidence Review — Ewing Sarcoma, Pesticides, and the Ladera Ranch Question

**Prepared:** 2026-07-18 · Hypothesis-neutral · Verified citations only (see
`literature_registry.csv`). Effect estimates are associations, not causation.

## 1. What is established about Ewing sarcoma etiology

- **A somatic gene fusion defines the disease.** ~85% of tumors carry the EWSR1-FLI1
  t(11;22) fusion (variant EWSR1-ETS fusions account for most of the rest). The fusion
  protein binds **GGAA microsatellites**; enhancer activity scales with the number of GGAA
  repeats. *(LIT-004, LIT-006)*
- **Germline susceptibility is real and central.** GWAS identify ≥6 loci, several with
  OR > 1.7; risk alleles lengthen GGAA microsatellites and amplify fusion-protein enhancer
  activity. *(LIT-004)*
- **The ancestry gradient is the single most striking epidemiologic fact, and it is largely
  biological.** Incidence is ~6–8× lower in African ancestry and ~2–3× lower in East/Southeast
  Asian ancestry than in European ancestry (IRRs 0.12–0.33); Pacific Islanders show elevated
  risk (IRR 2.18). This tracks germline GGAA-microsatellite architecture, not environment.
  *(LIT-003, LIT-005)*
- **The only firmly established "risk factors" are demographic:** European ancestry, male
  sex (~3:1), adolescence (peak 10–15). *(LIT-003, LIT-006)*

**Not established:** any environmental, lifestyle, radiation, or familial cause. Ewing
sarcoma is overwhelmingly sporadic; the fusion arises post-natally for unknown reasons. The
etiologically relevant exposure window, if one exists, is unknown.

**Hypothesis-neutral implication (critical for this project):** because Ewing sarcoma risk
is strongly ancestry-patterned through germline biology, a geographic cluster in a
predominantly non-Hispanic-white community has (a) an **elevated baseline expectation** and
(b) a plausible **non-environmental** explanation that must be modeled explicitly before any
excess is attributed to a local exposure. Using an all-population incidence rate as the
denominator would bias an SIR upward.

## 2. What is known about pesticides and pediatric cancer

**Supporting a contribution:**
- The strongest Ewing-specific positive signals are **parental farming** studies — Australia
  (farm residence OR 2.0; farming father OR 3.5) and a pooled/meta-analysis (paternal OR 2.3,
  maternal 3.9). Farming is a *proxy* for pesticide exposure, not a measurement. *(LIT-007,
  LIT-008)*
- For **other** childhood cancers, pesticide associations are more robust: leukemia
  (OR ~1.48), lymphoma (~1.53), brain tumors (1.22–1.49). These are consistent but are **not
  Ewing sarcoma.** *(LIT-010, LIT-020, LIT-011)*
- Chemical-specific: glyphosate is IARC 2A and a meta-analysis found +41% NHL in high
  exposure; 2,4-D is IARC 2B; permethrin is EPA "likely (if eaten)". *(LIT-017)*

**Weakening / not supporting it for Ewing specifically:**
- The **largest and most rigorous Ewing-relevant occupational study** (Great Britain, 5,369
  sarcoma cases) found **no** pesticide/agriculture association and could not replicate the
  farming signal; its only positive Ewing finding was textile dust. *(LIT-009)*
- **Both large California registry studies** — PM2.5 air pollution and oil/gas proximity —
  were **null overall**, with only non-significant, Hispanic-subgroup elevations. *(LIT-001,
  LIT-002)*
- The most-cited pesticide meta-analysis **does not report Ewing sarcoma at all.** *(LIT-010)*
- Regulators diverge: EPA/EFSA/ECHA classify glyphosate as *not* carcinogenic and glufosinate
  as "not likely"; the IARC labels are hazard identifications, not realistic-exposure risk
  conclusions. *(LIT-018)*

**Unresolved.** Whether pesticides contribute specifically to Ewing sarcoma is genuinely
unsettled: positive farming-proxy studies versus a null high-quality occupational study, all
built on small case counts and crude exposure surrogates.

## 3. Chemicals named in Ladera Ranch coverage — what the science actually says

- **Glufosinate-ammonium** (the "Lifeline"/"Attrimec"-associated active ingredient named by
  advocacy sources): EPA — **"not likely to be a human carcinogen."** **Not** IARC-classified.
  EU — classified for **presumed reproductive toxicity** with **neurotoxicity** signals, and
  **withdrawn/banned in the EU**. In-vitro genotoxicity reported but no regulatory cancer
  classification. **No study links glufosinate to Ewing sarcoma.** The "banned in N countries"
  figure comes from advocacy and needs independent confirmation.
- **Glyphosate:** IARC 2A vs EPA "not likely" — a real, ongoing scientific disagreement; NHL,
  not Ewing.
- **2,4-D** IARC 2B; **permethrin** EPA "likely (if eaten)"; **bifenthrin** EPA "possible";
  **imidacloprid** EPA Group E (no evidence). None linked to Ewing sarcoma.

## 4. Alternative hypotheses the literature supports investigating

1. **Chance aggregation in a high-baseline-risk population** — rare disease + elevated
   ancestry-specific expected count. *(LIT-003, LIT-005, LIT-012, LIT-016)*
2. **Germline genetic susceptibility / possible local ancestry enrichment.** *(LIT-004, LIT-005)*
3. **Post-hoc boundary / ascertainment artifact** — community-drawn boundary + case-finding.
   *(LIT-012)*
4. **Exposure misclassification from residential mobility.** *(LIT-019)*
5. **Air pollution / PM2.5** — plausible but weak/null direct ES evidence. *(LIT-002)*
6. **Legacy oil/gas infrastructure** — only environmental factor with even a suggestive ES
   signal, and only in Hispanic children; low prior for this community but worth mapping.
   *(LIT-001)*
7. **Diagnostic / reporting factors** — ascertainment or diagnostic substitution. *(LIT-003)*

## 5. Gaps and cautions

- **No study directly links Ewing sarcoma to glyphosate, glufosinate, 2,4-D, or any specific
  pyrethroid/neonicotinoid.** ES–pesticide evidence is entirely farming-proxy or dust-based.
- A widely-circulated "parental pesticide → Ewing OR = 2.01" figure **could not be verified**
  in a primary source and is **not cited** here. Do not use it.
- No single clean SEER age-specific pediatric ES rate (0–19, by ancestry) was extracted;
  pull directly from SEER*Explorer before computing any SIR.
- **No peer-reviewed cluster investigation of Ladera Ranch / Orange County Ewing sarcoma
  exists.** The closest precedents — Wake County NC, Washington County PA, McFarland CA — all
  **failed to confirm an environmental cause.**

**Bottom line (hypothesis-neutral):** Ewing sarcoma is a genetically/ancestry-driven,
sporadic cancer with no established environmental cause. Pesticide evidence for Ewing
specifically is weak, mixed, and proxy-based, with the highest-quality study null. A
defensible local analysis requires ancestry-specific expected rates, individual residence
histories, a pre-specified exposure hypothesis, and small-area exact/Bayesian statistics —
not a crude SIR drawn around a community-defined boundary.
