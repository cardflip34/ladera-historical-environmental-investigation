# Capistrano Unified School District — IPM & Pesticide Notification Findings

**Retrieved:** 2026-07-18. CUSD operates the schools serving Ladera Ranch. Partially closes
evidence gate **G08**.

## Primary source retrieved: CUSD Integrated Pest Management Plan

Publicly posted PDF (grade **A2**), text extracted directly. Key content:

- **District:** Capistrano Unified School District, 32972 Calle Perfecto, San Juan Capistrano,
  CA 92675.
- **IPM Coordinator:** Grounds Manager (District IPM Coordinator role), (949) 234-9527.
- **IPM Team:** includes a district **Pesticide Applicator Technician**.
- **IPM statement (paraphrased):** the district's goal is to implement IPM by focusing on
  long-term prevention or suppression of pests through accurate pest identification, frequent
  monitoring, appropriate action levels, and habitat modification using sanitation and
  mechanical/physical controls. Pesticides that are effective "will be used in a manner that
  minimizes risks to people, property and the environment and only after other options have
  been shown ineffective."
- **Scope:** IPM procedures cover **both structural and landscape pests**.
- **Contracting:** "CUSD may hire a contracted pest control company on an as needed basis."
  Before contracting, CUSD confirms the company understands Healthy Schools Act training and
  other requirements. **The plan does not name the contractor.**
- **Pest identification** is performed by the CUSD Pesticide Applicator Technician; monitoring
  and inspection are performed by site custodial, Maintenance & Operations, and Grounds staff,
  reported to the IPM Coordinator.

**What the plan does NOT contain:** any pesticide product names, active ingredients, EPA
registration numbers, application records, or site-specific data. Those live in a separate
document (below).

## Precise gap identified: the product list is linked but not publicly readable

CUSD's Notices & Resources page publicly links **"View the Annual Pesticide Notification and
Product List"** — the document that would name the products expected to be applied at CUSD
schools, including those serving Ladera Ranch. The link points to a Google Doc that
**requires a Google account sign-in to view**. It was therefore *not retrieved*; access
controls were respected and not circumvented.

This converts a vague gap ("school pesticide data not publicly queryable") into a **specific,
actionable request**: ask CUSD to supply the Annual Pesticide Notification and Product List in
an openly accessible format, which the Healthy Schools Act contemplates being made available
to parents and staff.

CUSD also offers a **72-hour advance pesticide application notification** registration for
parents, guardians, and employees (except emergency applications) — evidence that
site-specific application scheduling data exists and is distributed, and could be requested.

## Status of related items

- **Healthy Schools Act annual reports** are submitted by schools/districts to DPR by January
  30 each year; DPR does not publish a public searchable school-level database, so a direct
  request to DPR or CUSD is required.
- No CUSD statement on the Ladera Ranch cancer reports was located.

## Recommended request (added to evidence-gate package)

> Requesting, under the California Public Records Act: (1) the Annual Pesticide Notification
> and Product List for school years 2013–14 through 2025–26 in an openly accessible format;
> (2) Healthy Schools Act annual pesticide use reports submitted to DPR for the same years for
> school sites serving Ladera Ranch; (3) the identity of any contracted pest control business
> and the associated contracts; and (4) site-specific pesticide application records/notices
> for those school sites.
