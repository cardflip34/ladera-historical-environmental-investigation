## The honest reframe (so you don't waste a trip)
**The Sherman is the Newport Beach / Newport Bay repository — it is NOT the Irvine Ranch cattle-
operations archive.** None of its 56 catalogued collections is documented to hold cattle-dipping,
tick-quarantine, or veterinary records. Its value here is **land-title, land-use, and maps.** The
famous "45 volumes" is the **Parker Title Report on Tidelands in Newport Bay** (a 1979–87 tidelands
*title* study — land chain, not cattle). **The actual Irvine Ranch archive is at UC Irvine.**

## Sherman — what to pull if the library is open (prioritized)
1. **Miller California Land Grant Collection, 1810–1957** — *the best Sherman item.* Documents Rancho
   Lomas de Santiago & Rancho Santiago de Santa Ana (two of the three ranchos that became the Irvine
   Ranch), with GLO deeds, maps, surveys → the land-grant→patent→Irvine title chain and earliest
   boundary/water maps. Finding aid: oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/c8hx1f1p [A2]. *(Call no: ask staff.)*
2. **Aliso Gun Club Records, 1898–1972** — in-period (1898→) photos/registers from the **southwest
   Irvine Ranch**; marsh/water/land-use imagery of the same land & era. ark:/13030/c8dz0drq [A2].
3. **Parker Title Report (the 45 volumes)** — photocopied historical title docs, **maps/surveys, and
   source indexes** that point to primary records. No OAC finding aid — **ask staff for the call no.**
4. **Sherman Map & Book collections** (reading-room reference) — earliest regional/ranch maps showing
   water & structures; published Irvine Ranch histories that might name corrals/stock water. Search
   the **Sherman Digital Catalog** online before you go.

*Access:* register on arrival, non-circulating, appointment "strongly encouraged." Staff scans
$25/file (personal). Whether you may take your own reference photos — **ask.**

## UC Irvine Special Collections — the ACTUAL Irvine Ranch repository
Langson Library, 5th-floor reading room. **Appointment + material requests required ~7+ business days
ahead via Aeon.** Open Mon–Thu 9:00–3:00 (academic year). spcoll@uci.libanswers.edu · (949) 824-6836.
- **East Irvine Historic Resources Documentation Photographs, 1988 (MS-R 66)** — 230 photos of
  turn-of-the-century Irvine Ranch "hub" buildings incl. **garage & blacksmith shops**, worker
  houses. ark:/13030/tf900008z5 [A2].
- **Orange County Regional History Collection** — strengths explicitly include "agriculture… Irvine
  Ranch and The Irvine Company"; ask the archivist for the **COPH ranch-headquarters oral histories
  & archaeology (1999–2002)** and the "Baldwin"/San Joaquin Fruit & Investment / Frances Mutual Water
  Company records (stock water).

## ⭐ Orange County Archives — county records (Santa Ana) — NOW THE #1 TARGET
Old OC Courthouse, **Room 108, 211 W. Santa Ana Blvd.** Mon–Fri 9–12 & 1–4:30 · (714) 834-2536 ·
ocrecorder.com/oc-archives [A2].

**Why it jumped to #1 (from the records dig):** the definitive Irvine history (Cleland 1952) is
*silent* on dipping, so the county government files are now the best route. Ask specifically for:
- **Board of Supervisors minutes/proceedings, 1907–1912**, and the **county live-stock inspector /
  county veterinarian monthly reports** filed with them. The decisive analog: neighboring **San Luis
  Obispo's inspector reported "63 dipping vats in county"** to its Supervisors in 1908 — OC kept the
  same office (**county veterinarian Dr. W. S. McFarlane**, 1908) and almost certainly filed a
  comparable **vat count / location list**. This is the single document most likely to name OC vats.
- Any **cattle-tick quarantine / dipping-cost claims** (e.g., the 1908 Levengood v. County of Orange
  claim — dipping "by order of the county veterinarian").

Also worth a microfilm request via OC Public Library / UC: **Santa Ana Register / Santa Ana Blade,
1907–1912** (the county's own press, largely absent from the digitized CDNC — most likely to carry
granular vat detail).

*Context from the record:* the named 1908 OC dip sites are all **eastern/southern county** (Joplin
ranch/Trabuco Canyon, Capistrano, Yorba, Bixby/Santa Ana Canyon) — **none on Irvine land** — so these
county files, not the Irvine collections, are where an Irvine/San-Joaquin vat would be confirmed or
ruled out. See `research/oc_dipping_records/FINDINGS.md`.

## Questions to ask the archivist (any of the three)
1. Any **State Veterinarian, USDA Bureau of Animal Industry, tick-eradication, quarantine, or
   cattle-dipping** records touching the Irvine Ranch / Rancho San Joaquin, c.1900–1915?
2. Any **ranch maps or reports showing corrals, stock water, or dip/vat structures**? Any **photos of
   Bommer Canyon / cattle operations / stock ponds**?
3. (Sherman) Call number for the **Parker Title Report**, and can I see its **map/survey volumes**?
4. (OC Archives) Do **Board of Supervisors minutes / Ag Commissioner / County Veterinarian** records
   from ~1900–1915 mention **cattle dipping or tick quarantine** in the San Joaquin/Irvine area?
5. Where do the **Irvine Company's own historical cattle-operation records** (Bommer Canyon) reside?

## The single most important book to page-check
**Robert Glass Cleland, *The Irvine Ranch of Orange County, 1810–1950*** (Huntington Library, 1952).
The definitive ranch history; not surfaced online — check its index for dipping / vat / corral / tick.
