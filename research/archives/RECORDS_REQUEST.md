# Records request — Orange County historical cattle-tick dipping (1907–1912)

*A hand-to-staff request sheet. Independent historical land-use research; hypothesis-neutral. I am
looking for where cattle-dipping vats/stations were located in Orange County during the 1907–1912
tick quarantine — no contamination claim is being made.*

**Researcher:** ______________________  ·  **Date of visit:** ____________

---

## What I'm researching (one line for staff)
The Orange County cattle-tick eradication program, **c.1907–1912**, when arsenical dipping was
compulsory under the county veterinarian. I'm trying to find **the county's list/count and locations
of dipping vats**, especially in the **southwest/south county** (the area left under quarantine by the
**March 7, 1912** state proclamation: south-and-west of the Newport Beach → Newport-Tustin road →
Santa Ana-Trabuco road → Aliso Canyon road line).

---

## ⭐ Orange County Archives (Old Courthouse, Rm 108, Santa Ana) — primary
Please help me locate, for **1907–1912**:
1. **Board of Supervisors minutes / proceedings** — entries on **cattle-tick quarantine, dipping,
   the county veterinarian / livestock inspector, and dipping-cost claims.** *(A neighboring county's
   inspector reported "63 dipping vats in county" to its Supervisors in 1908 — I'm hoping Orange filed
   a comparable **vat count / location list**.)*
2. **County Veterinarian / County Livestock Inspector reports or correspondence** — the office held by
   **Dr. W. S. McFarlane** in 1908.
3. Any file on the **1908 Levengood claim/suit against the County of Orange** (cattle died after
   dipping "by order of the county veterinarian") — it may cite the dipping location.
4. **Santa Ana Register / Santa Ana Blade newspaper microfilm, 1907–1912** (if held) — the local press,
   not in the digitized statewide collection.

## California State Archives (Sacramento) — by request/mail if not visiting
- **Office of the State Veterinarian (Dr. Charles Keane) — quarantine case files & correspondence,
  1907–1912.** Specifically the enforcement actions the biennial reports mention: **quarantine-violation
  convictions in San Diego County (1909–10)** and a **lien suit in San Luis Obispo County (1907–08)** —
  these would name owners/ranches.

## The map to ask for (either archive or online)
- **6th Biennial Report of the State Veterinarian (for the two years ending June 30, 1912)** — it
  contains **two infestation map plates** (1904 and 1912) showing the quarantined areas. The 1912 plate
  graphically fixes the **southwest-Orange district.** *(Bound in the Appendix to the Journals of the
  Senate & Assembly, 40th Session, 1913, Vol. 2; also on Internet Archive `appendixtojourna19132cali`.)*

## Questions for the archivist
1. Do you hold **Board of Supervisors** or **county veterinarian/livestock-inspector** records naming
   **cattle-dipping vats or stations** in Orange County, ~1907–1912?
2. Any **maps** (county, road, or ranch) from ~1908–1912 showing **dipping stations, corrals, or stock

3. Where would the **county veterinarian's original dipping records** survive, if not here?
4. Do you have the **Santa Ana Register/Blade** for 1907–1912 on microfilm?

## Context I already have (so I don't ask for it)
Four named 1908 OC dip sites are already documented in the press — **J. C. Joplin ranch (Trabuco
Canyon), Capistrano, Yorba, and the Bixby ranch (Santa Ana Canyon)** — plus the Levengood herd near
Santa Ana. **No source yet names a vat in the central Irvine Ranch / Rancho San Joaquin.** I'm looking
to complete the county's own vat list and fix the southwest-district locations.
